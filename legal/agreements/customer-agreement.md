## 1. Introduction

This Customer Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between you ("Customer") and the Marketplace ("Platform") upon account registration. It governs the contractual relationship between the Customer and the Marketplace in connection with all services booked through the Platform.

This Agreement should be read alongside the Customer Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Payment Terms.

---

## 2. Scope of Agreement

By creating a customer account, you agree that:

- The Marketplace will facilitate connections between you and verified professionals
- All transactions and communications occur through the Marketplace platform
- The Marketplace acts as an intermediary and is not a party to the service contract between you and a professional
- Your rights and obligations are governed by this Agreement and applicable Marketplace policies

---

## 3. Customer Obligations

You agree to:

3.1 Provide accurate information at registration and during booking processes

3.2 Pay for all booked services through the Marketplace payment system only

3.3 Treat professionals with respect, courtesy, and professionalism

3.4 Provide a safe environment when services are performed at your property

3.5 Respond to professional communication within a reasonable timeframe

3.6 Promptly accept or dispute completed services within the 48-hour acceptance window

3.7 Not attempt to engage professionals outside of the Marketplace to circumvent platform fees

3.8 Comply with all applicable Marketplace policies, including the Customer Terms of Service

---

## 4. Marketplace Obligations

The Marketplace agrees to:

4.1 Provide a secure platform for booking and managing professional services

4.2 Facilitate payment processing and hold funds in escrow during service delivery

4.3 Provide access to verified professional profiles and service listings

4.4 Offer a dispute resolution process for service-related disagreements

4.5 Protect your personal data in accordance with the Privacy Policy

4.6 Provide responsive customer support

4.7 Notify you of material changes to Platform policies with reasonable notice

---

## 5. Payment and Escrow

5.1 Upon booking confirmation, payment is charged and held in escrow by the Marketplace

5.2 Escrow is released to the professional upon your acceptance of the completed service or expiry of the 48-hour acceptance window

5.3 If a dispute is raised within the acceptance window, funds are held pending resolution

5.4 Refunds are processed in accordance with the Refund Policy

---

## 6. Limitation of Marketplace Liability

The Marketplace is a facilitation platform and does not:

- Employ professionals or supervise service delivery
- Guarantee the quality, timing, or outcome of any service
- Accept liability for property damage, personal injury, or financial loss caused by a professional
- Warrant that professional profiles or reviews are fully accurate

The Marketplace's liability to you shall not exceed the amount paid for the specific booking giving rise to the claim.

---

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement:

- The Customer may close their account at any time, subject to resolution of active bookings
- The Marketplace may terminate this Agreement for breach of Customer Terms, fraudulent activity, or conduct harmful to other users

Upon termination, outstanding obligations (including payment for completed services) survive.
