# Partner Agreement

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Partner Agreement ("Agreement") governs the relationship between the Marketplace ("Platform") and any business, organisation, or individual ("Partner") entering into a formal partnership arrangement with the Marketplace.

Partners may include, but are not limited to:

- Referral and affiliate partners
- Technology integration partners
- Distribution partners
- Industry association partners
- Corporate or enterprise customers

---

## 2. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Partner** | The business or individual entering into this Agreement |
| **Partnership Services** | The services, referrals, integrations, or activities the Partner provides under this Agreement |
| **Marketplace Services** | The Platform's services as described in the Marketplace Terms of Service |
| **Partner Materials** | Any content, tools, or assets provided by the Partner for use by the Marketplace |

---

## 3. Scope of Partnership

3.1 The specific nature, scope, and terms of each partnership are defined in a separate Partnership Schedule or Statement of Work ("SOW") annexed to this Agreement.

3.2 This Agreement provides the baseline legal framework applicable to all partnership arrangements. The SOW governs commercial specifics.

3.3 In the event of conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, the SOW prevails for commercial terms; this Agreement prevails for legal and compliance terms.

---

## 4. Partner Obligations

The Partner agrees to:

4.1 Accurately represent the Marketplace, its services, and its policies in all communications with prospective users or customers

4.2 Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in performing Partnership Services

4.3 Not make warranties or representations about the Marketplace beyond those authorised in writing

4.4 Maintain the confidentiality of any proprietary Marketplace information disclosed in the course of the partnership

4.5 Not engage in any activity that conflicts with the Marketplace's interests or brings the Platform into disrepute

4.6 Maintain adequate records of all partnership-related activities for audit purposes

4.7 Comply with data protection laws when handling any personal data shared under this Agreement

---

## 5. Marketplace Obligations

The Marketplace agrees to:

5.1 Provide the Partner with the information, access, and materials necessary to perform Partnership Services

5.2 Pay any fees or commissions as specified in the applicable SOW

5.3 Credit the Partner's contributions in accordance with the SOW

5.4 Provide a dedicated partner contact for communication and support

---

## 6. Fees and Compensation

6.1 Compensation terms are specified in the SOW. Unless otherwise stated, no fees are payable under this Agreement alone.

6.2 Invoices must be submitted in the format and on the schedule specified in the SOW.

6.3 Payments will be made within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice, unless otherwise agreed.

6.4 The Marketplace reserves the right to withhold payment in the event of a good-faith dispute about the Partner's performance.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.

7.2 The Marketplace grants the Partner a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use its brand assets solely for the purposes authorised under this Agreement.

7.3 The Partner grants the Marketplace a licence to display Partner Materials for the duration of the partnership.

7.4 No intellectual property rights are transferred by this Agreement.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party may disclose confidential information to the other in the course of the partnership. Each party agrees to:

- Keep confidential information secret
- Use it only for the purposes of this Agreement
- Not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent
- Apply the same level of protection as to its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care

8.2 Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

8.3 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of 5 years.

---

## 9. Data Protection

9.1 Where either party processes personal data on behalf of the other, a Data Processing Agreement must be executed prior to any such processing.

9.2 Each party is responsible for its own compliance with applicable data protection laws.

9.3 Personal data shared under this Agreement may only be used for the purposes specified in this Agreement and any applicable DPA.

---

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 This Agreement commences on the date it is signed by both parties and continues until terminated.

10.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice.

10.3 Either party may terminate immediately for:

- Material breach not cured within 14 days of written notice
- Insolvency or bankruptcy of the other party
- Conduct that violates applicable law

10.4 Upon termination, each party must return or destroy confidential information belonging to the other.

---

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim, or (b) USD $10,000.

---

## 12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by applicable law as specified in the SOW, or where not specified, by the law of the Marketplace's principal jurisdiction.

---

## 13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with any SOWs and annexes, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the partnership and supersedes all prior agreements on the same subject.
