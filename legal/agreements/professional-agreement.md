**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Professional Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between you ("Professional") and the Marketplace ("Platform") upon successful completion of the professional registration and verification process. It governs the contractual relationship between the Professional and the Marketplace.

This Agreement should be read alongside the Professional Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Payment Terms.

---

## 2. Nature of Relationship

2.1 You are an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of the Marketplace.

2.2 You retain full control over how you deliver your services, including the tools, methods, and timing used.

2.3 You are responsible for all costs, taxes, licences, and regulatory compliance associated with your professional practice.

2.4 The Marketplace provides a platform for connecting you with customers and facilitating payments. It does not direct or supervise your work.

---

## 3. Professional Onboarding and Verification

3.1 You must complete the Marketplace's verification programme as a condition of access. This includes identity verification, qualification checks, and any category-specific requirements.

3.2 You warrant that all information submitted during onboarding is truthful, complete, and current.

3.3 You must promptly notify the Marketplace of any changes that affect your eligibility to operate on the Platform.

3.4 The Marketplace reserves the right to perform periodic re-verification and to suspend access pending verification completion.

---

## 4. Professional Obligations

You agree to:

4.1 Deliver services in a professional, competent, and timely manner consistent with the booking agreement

4.2 Maintain accurate, up-to-date service listings and availability calendars

4.3 Communicate with customers respectfully and promptly

4.4 Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and professional standards in the delivery of your services

4.5 Hold all required licences, certifications, and insurance for the services you offer

4.6 Not solicit customers to transact outside the Marketplace platform

4.7 Report any safety incidents, customer misconduct, or policy violations to the Marketplace

4.8 Comply with all Marketplace policies, including the Professional Terms of Service

---

## 5. Marketplace Obligations

The Marketplace agrees to:

5.1 Provide a secure platform for listing and delivering professional services

5.2 Process customer payments and release earnings to you in accordance with the Payment Terms

5.3 Provide access to dispute resolution processes

5.4 Protect your personal data in accordance with the Privacy Policy

5.5 Provide professional support and account assistance

5.6 Notify you of material policy changes with reasonable advance notice

---

## 6. Fees and Earnings

6.1 The Marketplace charges a service fee on each completed booking. The current fee schedule is available in your account settings.

6.2 Earnings are credited to your account upon customer acceptance of a completed service.

6.3 You may withdraw available earnings in accordance with the Withdrawal Policy.

6.4 The Marketplace reserves the right to withhold earnings subject to open disputes or suspected fraud.

---

## 7. Non-Solicitation

7.1 You agree not to directly solicit customers you met through the Marketplace to engage your services outside the Platform for a period of 12 months following the last completed booking with that customer.

7.2 Violation of this clause entitles the Marketplace to a circumvention fee equivalent to 20% of the estimated off-platform contract value, in addition to any other remedies available.

---

8.1 You retain ownership of content you create and submit to the Platform (profile descriptions, portfolio images, etc.).

8.2 You grant the Marketplace a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display, reproduce, and distribute your submitted content for the purpose of operating and promoting the Platform.

8.3 You warrant that submitted content does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

---

## 9. Suspension and Termination

9.1 The Marketplace may suspend your account pending investigation of complaints, disputes, or suspected misconduct.

9.2 The Marketplace may permanently terminate this Agreement for material breach, fraudulent conduct, failure to maintain verification, or repeated policy violations.

9.3 You may terminate this Agreement by closing your account, subject to resolution of all active bookings and outstanding earnings.

9.4 Obligations relating to non-solicitation, confidentiality, and dispute resolution survive termination.

---

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by applicable law in your jurisdiction of registration, subject to the dispute resolution provisions in the Marketplace Terms of Service.

The Marketplace may amend this Agreement from time to time. Notice will be provided via email or platform notification. Continued use of the Platform following the effective date constitutes acceptance.
