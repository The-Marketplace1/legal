# Vendor Agreement

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") governs the relationship between the Marketplace ("Platform") and any third-party supplier, service provider, or technology vendor ("Vendor") providing goods, services, or software to the Marketplace.

---

## 2. Vendor Obligations

The Vendor agrees to:

2.1 Deliver the goods or services described in the applicable Purchase Order or Statement of Work ("SOW") to the agreed specification, timeline, and quality standard

2.2 Maintain adequate resources, personnel, and systems to meet its obligations

2.3 Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards

2.4 Maintain appropriate insurance coverage throughout the term of this Agreement

2.5 Not subcontract obligations without prior written consent from the Marketplace

2.6 Promptly notify the Marketplace of any issues likely to affect delivery

2.7 Comply with the Marketplace's data protection, security, and ethical standards

---

## 3. Marketplace Obligations

The Marketplace agrees to:

3.1 Provide timely access to information, systems, and personnel the Vendor requires to perform its obligations

3.2 Pay undisputed invoices within the agreed payment terms

3.3 Notify the Vendor promptly of any changes that affect the scope of work

---

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 Fees are specified in the applicable SOW or Purchase Order.

4.2 Invoices must be submitted in the specified format. Payment terms are net 30 days from receipt of a valid invoice unless otherwise agreed.

4.3 The Marketplace may dispute invoices in good faith. Disputed amounts are withheld pending resolution. Undisputed amounts remain payable on schedule.

4.4 The Marketplace is not liable for any fees not agreed in writing in advance.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 All deliverables created by the Vendor specifically for the Marketplace ("Work Product") are assigned to the Marketplace upon full payment.

5.2 The Vendor retains ownership of pre-existing tools, frameworks, and methodologies, but grants the Marketplace a perpetual licence to use them to the extent embedded in the deliverables.

5.3 The Vendor warrants that deliverables do not infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Vendor must keep all Marketplace confidential information strictly confidential and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement.

6.2 The Vendor must ensure its personnel and subcontractors are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

6.3 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 5 years.

---

## 7. Data Protection and Security

7.1 Where the Vendor processes personal data on behalf of the Marketplace, the parties must execute a Data Processing Agreement prior to any processing.

7.2 The Vendor must implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

7.3 The Vendor must notify the Marketplace of any security breach or suspected breach within 24 hours of discovery.

7.4 The Vendor must comply with the Marketplace's information security requirements as provided from time to time.

---

## 8. Warranties

The Vendor warrants that:

8.1 It has the authority to enter into this Agreement

8.2 The goods or services will conform to the agreed specifications

8.3 Its personnel are qualified and experienced in the relevant field

8.4 Its systems and software do not contain malware or security vulnerabilities known at the time of delivery

---

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days' written notice.

9.2 The Marketplace may terminate immediately for material breach, insolvency, or conduct that poses a risk to the Platform or its users.

9.3 Upon termination, the Vendor must return or destroy all Marketplace confidential information and data.

---

## 10. Limitation of Liability

The Vendor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

---

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by applicable law as agreed in the relevant SOW.
