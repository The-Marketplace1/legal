# Call Recording Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when Marketplace calls may be recorded, how participants are informed, how consent is managed, and how recordings are protected and deleted.

## 2. Recording Principles

The Marketplace records calls only where there is a documented purpose, lawful basis, and operational need. Potential purposes include:

- Safety and abuse investigation
- Fraud prevention
- Dispute resolution
- Customer support quality and training, where permitted
- Legal or regulatory compliance

Recording is not enabled for every call and may vary by country, call type, and provider.

## 3. Notice and Consent

Before recording begins, participants receive a clear notice that:

- The call may be recorded
- The purpose of recording
- The applicable privacy notice
- How to decline recording or use an alternative channel, where required

Where the law requires consent, recording starts only after the required consent is obtained. If a participant does not consent, the call may end or move to text support where appropriate.

The Marketplace maintains records of consent, notice, withdrawal, and the jurisdictional rule applied.

## 4. Local Law

Call-recording laws vary by jurisdiction. Before enabling recording in a country or region, Legal and Privacy must confirm:

- Whether one-party or all-party consent applies
- Required notice wording and timing
- Whether recording is permitted for the intended purpose
- Cross-border transfer and processor requirements
- Any sector-specific restrictions

The stricter lawful requirement applies where jurisdiction is uncertain.

## 5. Access and Use

Recordings are confidential and may be accessed only by authorised personnel for the documented purpose. Recordings must not be:

- Shared publicly
- Used for unrelated marketing
- Downloaded to unauthorised personal devices
- Used to make a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect

## 6. Retention and Deletion

Standard call recordings are deleted or anonymised after **90 days**, unless a longer period is required for an active dispute, safety investigation, legal hold, regulatory obligation, or consent record. Metadata follows the retention schedule in the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Policy.

Legal or compliance holds must identify the matter, data scope, owner, and release date or review date.

## 7. Data Subject Rights

Participants may request access, deletion, restriction, or other applicable rights by contacting privacy@marketplace.com. Rights may be limited where disclosure would affect another person's privacy, an investigation, or a legal obligation.

## 8. Third-Party Providers

Communication providers must execute a Data Processing Agreement, meet the Marketplace's security requirements, and support the Marketplace's notice, rights, deletion, and incident obligations.
