# Communication Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs privacy and lawful use of communication features made available through the Marketplace, including chat, in-platform calls, VoIP, email notifications, and masked phone numbers.

It supplements the Privacy Policy and applies to customers, professionals, partners, and Marketplace personnel.

## 2. Communication Channels

The Marketplace may provide:

- In-platform text messaging
- Voice or VoIP calls
- Masked telephone numbers that prevent direct disclosure of personal numbers
- Booking, safety, and support notifications

Feature availability depends on the user's region, account status, booking status, and applicable law.

## 3. Privacy Expectations

Users must use Marketplace communication tools only for legitimate booking, service, safety, and support purposes. Users must not:

- Share another person's private information without authorisation
- Record, forward, publish, or reproduce communications unlawfully
- Use a masked number to evade safety controls or contact restrictions
- Harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, or sexually harass another person
- Solicit off-platform transactions in violation of the Marketplace Rules

Users should assume that communications may be retained and reviewed for safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, service quality, and legal compliance as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Monitoring and Review

The Marketplace may use automated tools and authorised human review to:

- Detect fraud, abuse, threats, and prohibited solicitation
- Investigate reports and disputes
- Protect users and maintain platform integrity
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations

Access to communication content is limited to authorised personnel with a documented business or legal need. The Marketplace does not sell private communications.

## 5. Calls and VoIP

Calls may be routed through a Marketplace or third-party communication provider. Call metadata may include participants, timestamps, duration, routing information, and technical diagnostics. Content is handled only as described in the Call Recording Policy and Privacy Policy.

## 6. Masked Numbers

Masked numbers are temporary communication identifiers. They do not create a private, permanent line and should not be used for emergencies. Users should contact local emergency services directly in an emergency.

The Marketplace may disable a masked number after a booking, account suspension, safety report, or expiry of the operational retention period.

## 7. User Reports

Report abusive, unsafe, or suspicious communication through the platform or:

- **Safety:** safety@marketplace.com
- **Privacy:** privacy@marketplace.com
- **Security:** security@marketplace.com
