# Communication Retention Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets retention periods for Marketplace chat, calls, VoIP records, masked-number metadata, and notification preferences. Retention is limited to what is necessary for service delivery, safety, disputes, security, and legal compliance.

## 2. Retention Schedule

| Record | Standard Retention | Exception |
|---|---:|---|
| In-platform chat content | 3 years from last activity | Legal hold, active dispute, or safety investigation |
| Call recordings | 90 days | Active investigation, dispute, or legal obligation |
| Call metadata | 3 years | Longer where required for tax, fraud, or legal claims |
| Masked-number routing logs | 1 year | Security or law-enforcement request |
| Communication preferences and consent | Until changed or withdrawn + 3 years | Legal accountability requirement |
| Support correspondence | 3 years from closure | Active complaint or legal hold |
| Automated moderation events | 2 years | Active investigation or regulatory obligation |

## 3. Retention Controls

The responsible service owner must ensure:

- Retention periods are implemented consistently
- Deletion jobs are monitored and exceptions are documented
- Data is anonymised where it is still useful for aggregated reporting
- Access is limited during the retention period
- Legal holds override routine deletion only for the defined scope

## 4. Legal Holds

The Legal Team may issue a legal hold when information may be relevant to litigation, regulatory investigation, law enforcement, or a serious safety incident. A hold must be documented and reviewed at least every 90 days.

When a hold is released, normal deletion resumes. The release must be recorded.

## 5. Data Subject Requests

Requests to access or delete communication records are handled under the Privacy Policy, Data Processing Policy, and applicable regional law. A request does not require deletion where retention is legally required or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 6. Third-Party Providers

Providers must support the Marketplace's retention schedule and provide deletion confirmation when their engagement ends or the applicable period expires.
