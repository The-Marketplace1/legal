## 1. How to Use This Checklist

This checklist is a control aid for launch reviews, annual compliance reviews, new-country entry, and material product or policy changes. It does not replace legal advice or a jurisdiction-specific assessment.

The reviewer must record:

- Review date: ___________________
- Review scope: ___________________
- Jurisdictions: ___________________
- Review owner: ___________________
- Legal approver: ___________________
- Evidence location: ___________________

Mark each item **Complete**, **Not applicable**, or **Action required**. Every action required must have an owner and due date.

## 2. Governance and Legal Documents

- [ ] Marketplace Terms are current and approved
- [ ] Customer Terms and Customer Agreement are current
- [ ] Professional Terms and Professional Agreement are current
- [ ] Partner and Vendor Agreements are current
- [ ] Payment, Escrow, Refund, and Withdrawal Policies are current
- [ ] Dispute, Resolution, and Escalation Policies are current
- [ ] Safety and Marketplace Rules are current
- [ ] Version, effective date, and review date appear on each controlled document
- [ ] Material changes have an approval record and change-history entry

## 3. Privacy and Data Protection

- [ ] Privacy Policy is published and accessible before data collection
- [ ] Cookie Policy and consent controls match the documented purposes
- [ ] Data Processing Policy and retention schedules are current
- [ ] Record of Processing Activities is current
- [ ] Data subject request process is operational and tested
- [ ] Approved processor register is current
- [ ] Data Processing Agreements are signed with relevant processors
- [ ] International transfers have a documented lawful mechanism
- [ ] High-risk processing has a completed Privacy Impact Assessment or DPIA
- [ ] Data breach contacts and notification procedures are current

## 4. Regional Requirements

- [ ] Each operating country has an identified legal owner
- [ ] Country-specific consumer and data protection requirements are documented
- [ ] Required registrations, licences, and filings are current
- [ ] Regulator contacts are verified
- [ ] Regional privacy notices and consent language are approved
- [ ] Data localisation requirements have been assessed
- [ ] Tax, payments, and marketplace obligations have been assessed

## 5. Professionals and Service Safety

- [ ] Professional identity verification criteria are documented
- [ ] Regulated service categories have licence and qualification requirements
- [ ] Verification evidence access is limited and retained appropriately
- [ ] Re-verification and expiry controls are documented
- [ ] Adverse verification decisions have a review or appeal path
- [ ] Customer and professional safety reporting channels are operational
- [ ] Prohibited activities and content rules are published
- [ ] Safety incident escalation and law-enforcement referral criteria are documented

## 6. Payments and Disputes

- [ ] Payment and escrow terms match the customer and professional experience
- [ ] Refund and cancellation rules are consistent across documents
- [ ] Withdrawal eligibility and payout requirements are published
- [ ] Dispute timelines, evidence requirements, and appeal rights are current
- [ ] Manual financial actions have approval and audit controls
- [ ] Chargeback and fraud escalation responsibilities are documented

## 7. Security and Communications

- [ ] Security Policy and vulnerability response contacts are current
- [ ] Responsible Disclosure Policy is published
- [ ] Incident Response Policy includes current notification obligations
- [ ] Chat, VoIP, masked-number, and call-recording notices are current
- [ ] Call-recording rules have been checked for every enabled jurisdiction
- [ ] Communication retention and deletion controls are tested
- [ ] Admin access and internal control reviews are current
