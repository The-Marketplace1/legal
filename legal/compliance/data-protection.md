# Data Protection Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This Data Protection Policy sets out the Marketplace's binding obligations for protecting personal data across all jurisdictions in which it operates. It applies to all processing activities, all team members, and all systems that handle personal data.

This policy is aligned with:

- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- UK GDPR
- Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)
- Other applicable national data protection laws

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All personal data collected from or about customers, professionals, visitors, and employees
- All Marketplace systems, products, and services that process personal data
- All employees, contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of the Marketplace
- All data processing activities, whether automated or manual

---

## 3. Core Data Protection Obligations

### 3.1 Lawful Processing

All personal data must be processed on a valid legal basis. Unlawful processing is prohibited. See the Data Processing Policy for a full list of legal bases and when each applies.

### 3.2 Transparency

Data subjects must be informed of how their data is processed through clear, accessible privacy notices at the point of collection or as soon as practicable.

### 3.3 Purpose Limitation

Personal data collected for one purpose must not be repurposed for an incompatible use without a new legal basis or consent.

### 3.4 Data Minimisation

Only personal data that is strictly necessary for the stated purpose should be collected, stored, and processed. Teams must challenge any proposed data collection that does not have a clear, proportionate justification.

### 3.5 Accuracy

Personal data must be accurate and kept up to date. Processes must include mechanisms for data subjects to correct inaccurate data and for the Marketplace to update records proactively.

### 3.6 Storage Limitation

Personal data must be deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed. See the Data Processing Policy for full retention schedules.

### 3.7 Security

Appropriate technical and organisational security measures must be in place to protect personal data from loss, theft, unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction.

### 3.8 Accountability

The Marketplace must be able to demonstrate compliance with these principles at all times.

---

## 4. Special Categories of Personal Data

The following categories of personal data are classified as sensitive and require heightened protection:

- Racial or ethnic origin
- Political opinions
- Religious or philosophical beliefs
- Trade union membership
- Genetic data
- Biometric data (for the purpose of uniquely identifying a person)
- Health data
- Sexual orientation or sex life
- Criminal convictions or offences

Processing of special category data is permitted only where a specific exemption or condition applies (e.g., explicit consent, employment law obligations, vital interests).

All processing of special category data must be documented and approved by the Data Protection Officer.

---

## 5. Data Subject Rights

The Marketplace is committed to honouring all data subject rights under applicable law:

| Right | What It Means | Our Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Receive a copy of personal data held | Within 30 days |
| Rectification | Correct inaccurate data | Within 30 days |
| Erasure | Delete data where eligible | Within 30 days |
| Restriction | Pause processing | Immediately on valid request |
| Portability | Receive data in machine-readable format | Within 30 days |
| Objection | Object to legitimate interests or marketing | Immediately for marketing |
| Withdraw consent | Revoke consent | Immediately |

All requests are handled through privacy@marketplace.com and logged in the Data Subject Request Register.

---

## 6. Third-Party Data Processors

Before engaging any third party that will process personal data on behalf of the Marketplace:

1. A due diligence assessment of the processor's data protection practices must be conducted
2. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) must be signed
3. The processor must be added to the Approved Processor Register
4. Sub-processing by the processor requires prior written approval

The Legal and Engineering teams maintain the Approved Processor Register jointly.

---

## 7. International Transfers

Personal data may only be transferred to countries outside the EEA/UK/Nigeria where:

- The country has been deemed adequate by the relevant authority, OR
- Appropriate safeguards are in place (SCCs, IDTAs, BCRs), AND
- A transfer impact assessment is completed for high-risk transfers

International transfer decisions must be approved by the DPO.

---

## 8. Data Breach Management

All suspected or confirmed data breaches must be reported to the DPO and the Security Team immediately. The Marketplace's full breach management process is set out in:

`security/incident-response-policy.md`

Key obligations:

- Internal breach notification within 1 hour of discovery
- Regulatory notification within 72 hours (where required)
- Data subject notification without undue delay (where high risk)
- Full breach documentation regardless of severity

---

## 9. Privacy by Design

All new products, features, or processing activities must incorporate data protection from inception:

- A Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) must be completed before launch
- Privacy-preserving options must be the default settings
- Only the minimum necessary data should be collected
- Technical security controls must be in place before go-live

---

## 10. Employee Obligations

All Marketplace personnel with access to personal data must:

- Complete data protection training at onboarding and annually
- Handle personal data only for authorised purposes
- Report any suspected breaches or policy violations immediately
- Sign and abide by confidentiality obligations
- Not access personal data beyond what their role requires

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination.

---

## 11. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and updated following significant legal, technical, or operational changes. The DPO is responsible for initiating reviews.
