## 1. Introduction

This document describes how the Marketplace complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the UK GDPR (as retained in UK law by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and amended by the Data Protection, Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2019).

It applies to all personal data processing involving EU and UK data subjects, regardless of where processing takes place.

---

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

### 2.1 Data Controller

The Marketplace acts as a **data controller** for personal data collected from its users (customers, professionals, visitors) in determining the purposes and means of processing.

### 2.2 Data Processor

The Marketplace acts as a **data processor** when processing personal data on behalf of professional users who operate as data controllers in their own right (e.g., when a professional stores client notes through the platform).

### 2.3 Data Protection Officer (DPO)

The Marketplace has appointed a Data Protection Officer:

**DPO Contact:** privacy@marketplace.com

The DPO is responsible for:

- Advising on GDPR obligations
- Monitoring compliance
- Acting as the contact point for supervisory authorities
- Handling data subject rights requests

## 3. Legal Bases Under GDPR

The Marketplace relies on the following GDPR legal bases for processing:

| Processing Activity | Legal Basis (GDPR Article) |
|---|---|
| Account creation and management | Article 6(1)(b) — Contractual necessity |
| Booking and payment processing | Article 6(1)(b) — Contractual necessity |
| Professional identity verification | Article 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation; Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests |
| Fraud prevention and security | Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests |
| Marketing communications | Article 6(1)(a) — Consent |
| Analytics and service improvement | Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interests |
| Regulatory compliance and audit | Article 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation |
| Background check results (special category) | Article 9(2)(b) — Employment / legal obligation; Article 9(2)(a) — Explicit consent |

Legitimate interests are balanced against data subjects' rights and freedoms. A Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) is conducted for all processing based on this ground.

## 4. Data Subject Rights Under GDPR

EU and UK data subjects have the following rights, which the Marketplace honours:

### 4.1 Right to Be Informed (Articles 13–14)

We provide transparent notice of our processing activities through the Privacy Policy displayed at account registration and publicly available on the platform.

### 4.2 Right of Access (Article 15)

Data subjects may request a copy of all personal data held about them. Requests are fulfilled within **30 days** at no charge (with the ability to extend by 2 months for complex requests, with notice).

### 4.3 Right to Rectification (Article 16)

Data subjects may request correction of inaccurate personal data. Requests are fulfilled within **30 days**.

### 4.4 Right to Erasure / "Right to Be Forgotten" (Article 17)

Data subjects may request deletion of their personal data. Deletion is performed except where:

- Retention is required by law (e.g., tax records for 7 years)
- Data is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims
- Processing is necessary for the performance of a task in the public interest

### 4.5 Right to Restriction (Article 18)

Data subjects may request that processing be restricted while accuracy is contested, the legal basis is challenged, or an objection is pending.

### 4.6 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

Data subjects may receive their personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON or CSV) and may request transmission to another controller where technically feasible.

### 4.7 Right to Object (Article 21)

Data subjects may object to:

- Processing based on legitimate interests — we will cease unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds
- Direct marketing — we will cease immediately with no grounds needed

### 4.8 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)

Where the Marketplace uses fully automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects, data subjects have the right to:

- Obtain human review of the decision
- Contest the decision
- Receive an explanation of the logic involved

The Marketplace does not currently use solely automated decision-making for eligibility or enforcement decisions.

## 5. International Data Transfers

When transferring EU/UK personal data outside the EEA/UK, the Marketplace uses appropriate transfer mechanisms:

| Transfer Mechanism | Application |
|---|---|
| Adequacy decisions | Transfers to countries with an adequacy decision from the European Commission |
| Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) | Transfers to third countries where no adequacy decision exists |
| UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) | UK data transfers |
| Binding Corporate Rules | Where applicable for intra-group transfers |

Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) are conducted for high-risk transfers.

---

## 6. Records of Processing Activities (ROPA)

The Marketplace maintains a Record of Processing Activities as required by Article 30, which includes:

- Purpose of each processing activity
- Categories of data and data subjects
- Recipients and transfers
- Security measures
- Retention periods

The ROPA is available to supervisory authorities upon request.

---

## 7. Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)

DPIAs are conducted before commencing any processing likely to result in a high risk to data subjects' rights and freedoms, including:

- Systematic profiling with legal or significant effects
- Large-scale processing of special category data
- Systematic monitoring of publicly accessible areas
- New technologies with unknown risk profiles

Results of DPIAs are documented and the DPO consulted.

---

## 8. Data Breach Notification

Under GDPR Articles 33–34:

| Obligation | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Notify supervisory authority (if high risk) | Within 72 hours of becoming aware |
| Notify affected data subjects (if high risk to rights) | Without undue delay |
| Document all breaches | Immediately, regardless of severity |

See `security/incident-response-policy.md` for the full breach response procedure.

---

## 9. Privacy by Design and Default (Article 25)

The Marketplace implements data protection by design and by default:

- Only data necessary for the specified purpose is collected (minimisation)
- Default settings are privacy-protective
- All new systems and features undergo privacy review
- Data protection requirements are embedded in development standards

---

## 10. Supervisory Authority

The Marketplace's lead supervisory authority for GDPR purposes is identified based on its EU establishment. UK activities are supervised by the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Data subjects may lodge complaints with:

- The supervisory authority in their EU member state of habitual residence or work
- The ICO for UK matters: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)
