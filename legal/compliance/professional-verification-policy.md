# Professional Verification Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the Marketplace's minimum requirements for verifying professionals before they may offer or accept services. Verification supports trust and safety; it is not a guarantee of service quality, outcome, or suitability.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every professional account and to any authorised third party that performs identity, qualification, licence, or background checks for the Marketplace.

## 3. Verification Stages

### 3.1 Identity Verification

Professionals must provide:

- Legal name and date of birth
- Government-issued identity document
- A live identity check or equivalent confirmation where required
- Current address or service-area information

The name on a payout account must match the verified professional or an approved registered business.

### 3.2 Qualification and Licence Verification

Where a service is regulated or requires specialist competence, the professional must provide:

- Relevant qualification or certification
- Licence, registration, or membership number
- Issuing body and expiry date
- Evidence of renewal when requested

The Marketplace may verify information directly with the issuing body or an approved verification provider.

### 3.3 Business Verification

Business professionals may be asked for:

- Registered business name and registration number
- Registered address
- Beneficial ownership information where required for compliance
- Tax or payment information required by law

### 3.4 Background Checks

Background checks may be required for a service category or jurisdiction where legally permitted and proportionate. The Marketplace obtains required notices and consent, uses authorised providers, and provides legally required rights and review mechanisms.

The Marketplace does not collect or use criminal-record information unless there is a documented lawful basis and a defined safety or compliance purpose.

## 4. Review and Approval

Verification outcomes may be:

- **Approved:** The professional may use the features permitted for the verified category
- **Pending:** More information or review is required
- **Restricted:** Only specified services or regions are available
- **Rejected:** The requirements were not met or the information could not be verified

Approval decisions are based on documented criteria applied consistently and proportionately. The Marketplace may use human review for complex or adverse decisions.

## 5. Ongoing Duties

Professionals must:

- Keep all verification information accurate and current
- Renew licences and certifications before expiry
- Notify the Marketplace of a suspension, revocation, or material change
- Complete re-verification when requested
- Not allow another person to use the verified account

## 6. Verification Removal and Suspension

Verification may be suspended or removed if:

- Information is false, incomplete, expired, or no longer verifiable
- A licence or qualification is revoked or suspended
- A safety, fraud, or compliance investigation requires a temporary restriction
- The professional fails to complete re-verification

The Marketplace may take immediate protective action where users face a material risk. Subject to law and safety considerations, the professional will receive the reason, the relevant evidence category, and an opportunity to appeal.

## 7. Data Protection

Verification data is processed under the Privacy Policy and Data Protection Policy. Access is restricted to authorised personnel and approved processors with a need to know. Documents are retained only for the period in the approved retention schedule or as required by law.

## 8. Verification Badges

A verification badge means only that the stated checks were completed at a particular time. It does not mean that the Marketplace endorses a professional, guarantees their work, or continuously monitors every credential.

## 9. Appeals

Professionals may appeal an adverse verification decision by contacting professionals@marketplace.com within 30 days. The appeal should identify any factual error and include new supporting evidence where available.
