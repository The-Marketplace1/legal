# Regional Requirements

This directory contains country- or region-specific legal requirements that supplement the global Marketplace policies.

## Rules for Regional Documents

- A regional document supplements, and does not silently override, a global policy
- The affected country or region must be explicit in the title and scope
- Each document must identify its legal source and last legal review
- Conflicts between regional and global requirements are escalated to Legal before publication
- New regional documents must be added to the repository index and change history

## Current Documents

- [`ndpa.md`](ndpa.md) — Nigeria Data Protection Act requirements

## Planned Coverage

Additional country documents are created only after the Marketplace confirms that it operates in, targets, or processes data from the relevant jurisdiction. Legal must approve the scope before a regional file is added.
