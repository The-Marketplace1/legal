# Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) Compliance

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This document describes how the Marketplace complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), which is administered by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). It applies to all processing of personal data of Nigerian data subjects, wherever that processing takes place.

---

## 2. Applicability

The NDPA applies to the Marketplace because it:

- Offers services to individuals in Nigeria
- Processes personal data of Nigerian residents
- Operates infrastructure or services from within Nigeria

---

## 3. Data Controller Registration

The Marketplace registers with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as a data controller and data processor as required by the NDPA.

- **Registration:** Completed with the NDPC prior to or upon commencement of data processing in Nigeria
- **Annual Filing:** The Marketplace completes its annual Data Protection Compliance Filing as required
- **Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO):** The Marketplace engages a DPCO-licensed compliance partner where required

---

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing Under the NDPA

Processing of personal data of Nigerian data subjects must rely on one or more of the following bases under the NDPA:

| Legal Basis | When Used |
|---|---|
| Consent | Marketing; optional data collection |
| Contract performance | Booking facilitation, payment processing |
| Legal obligation | Tax compliance, regulatory reporting |
| Vital interests | Emergency safety situations |
| Legitimate interests | Fraud prevention; platform security |

Consent under the NDPA must be:

- Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous
- Provided through a clear affirmative action
- Withdrawable at any time without detriment

---

## 5. Data Subject Rights Under the NDPA

Nigerian data subjects have the following rights under the NDPA:

| Right | Our Obligation |
|---|---|
| Right to be informed | Provide clear privacy notice at point of collection |
| Right of access | Provide a copy of personal data held within 30 days |
| Right to rectification | Correct inaccurate data within 30 days |
| Right to erasure | Delete data where eligible |
| Right to restrict processing | Pause processing on valid request |
| Right to object | Object to processing based on legitimate interests |
| Right to withdraw consent | Withdraw at any time |
| Right to data portability | Provide data in machine-readable format |

Rights requests are handled through privacy@marketplace.com and fulfilled within **30 days** (or as required under NDPA timelines).

---

## 6. Privacy Notice Requirements

Privacy notices provided to Nigerian data subjects must include:

- Identity and contact details of the data controller
- Purpose and legal basis for processing
- Categories of data collected
- Recipients of data
- International transfer information
- Retention period
- Data subject rights and how to exercise them
- Right to lodge a complaint with the NDPC

---

## 7. Data Localisation

Where Nigerian law requires that certain categories of data be stored on servers within Nigeria, the Marketplace will comply with applicable data localisation requirements. The Marketplace's engineering and legal teams jointly assess localisation obligations on an ongoing basis.

---

## 8. International Data Transfers

Transfers of Nigerian personal data outside Nigeria require:

- An adequacy determination (where the NDPC has issued one), OR
- Appropriate safeguards (standard data protection clauses approved by the NDPC), AND
- Written approval where required by the NDPC

All international transfers of Nigerian data are documented and assessed prior to implementation.

---

## 9. Data Breach Notification

Under the NDPA:

| Obligation | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Notify the NDPC | Within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach |
| Notify affected data subjects | Without undue delay where high risk |
| Document all breaches | Immediately, regardless of severity |

See `security/incident-response-policy.md` for the full breach response process.

---

## 10. Data Protection Impact Assessments

For processing activities that are likely to result in high risk to Nigerian data subjects, the Marketplace conducts a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) prior to commencement, in line with NDPA requirements.

---

## 11. Third-Party Processors in Nigeria

All third-party processors operating in Nigeria or processing Nigerian data on behalf of the Marketplace must:

- Sign a Data Processing Agreement compliant with NDPA requirements
- Demonstrate adequate data protection practices
- Be registered with the NDPC where required
