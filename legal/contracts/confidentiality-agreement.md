## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore or engage in a business relationship / service engagement ("Purpose") in connection with which the Disclosing Party may share certain confidential information with the Receiving Party. The Parties agree that such information must be protected.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether in writing, orally, electronically, or by any other means, that is:

- Marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary", or
- Reasonably understood to be confidential given the context of disclosure

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- Business plans, strategies, and financial information
- Customer and client data
- Technical processes, software, and source code
- Trade secrets and proprietary methods
- Personnel information
- Any information related to service delivery under a Marketplace booking

---

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

3.1 Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement

3.2 Was rightfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure

3.3 Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information

3.4 Is disclosed with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party

3.5 Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (with prompt prior written notice to the Disclosing Party where legally permitted)

---

## 4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

4.1 Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential

4.2 Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose described above

4.3 Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party

4.4 Limit access to Confidential Information to its employees or agents who have a need to know and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations

4.5 Apply at least the same degree of care to protect Confidential Information as it applies to its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care

4.6 Promptly notify the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any actual or suspected unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information

---

## 5. Return or Destruction of Information

Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, or upon termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall:

- Return all Confidential Information in tangible form, and
- Delete or destroy all digital copies of Confidential Information

The Receiving Party shall certify in writing that it has complied with this obligation.

---

## 6. Term

This Agreement commences on the date above and continues for **[2 years / 3 years / as agreed]** unless terminated earlier by written agreement of both Parties.

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for **[3 years / 5 years / as agreed]** after the date of termination.

---

## 7. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available at law.

---

## 8. No Licence

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any licence or right to use the Disclosing Party's intellectual property, except as strictly necessary for the Purpose.

---

## 9. No Obligation to Disclose

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to disclose any Confidential Information. Either Party may decline to share specific information at its discretion.

---
