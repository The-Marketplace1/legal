# Freelancer Contract Template

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

> **Usage Note:** This template is used for formalising the engagement between a professional (freelancer) and a customer for services booked through the Marketplace. Where a booking is confirmed through the Marketplace platform, the platform booking confirmation — combined with the Professional Terms of Service and Customer Terms of Service — constitutes the binding agreement. This template is provided for use in contexts where a separate written contract is required (e.g., large projects, regulated sectors, or customer request).

## 3. Independent Contractor Status

3.1 The Professional is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Customer or the Marketplace.

3.2 The Professional is responsible for all taxes, insurance, licences, and regulatory compliance applicable to their business.

3.3 The Customer has no obligation to provide tools, equipment, or workspace unless agreed in writing.

---

## 4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this Contract are assigned to the Customer upon full payment.

4.2 The Professional retains ownership of pre-existing tools, methods, templates, and background IP used in delivering the Services. A licence to use such IP embedded in deliverables is granted to the Customer.

4.3 The Professional warrants that deliverables do not infringe third-party intellectual property rights.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 The Professional agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the Customer in the course of this engagement.

5.2 Confidential information may not be disclosed to third parties or used for any purpose other than performing the Services.

5.3 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Contract for a period of **3 years**.

---

## 6. Warranties

The Professional warrants that:

- They have the skills, qualifications, and authority to perform the Services
- The Services will be delivered in a professional manner consistent with industry standards
- All deliverables will be original (or properly licenced) and free from defects at the time of delivery

---

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this Contract will be handled through the Marketplace's dispute resolution process as described in the Dispute Policy. Both parties agree to engage in the Marketplace's process in good faith before seeking external resolution.

---

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Contract with reasonable written notice. In the event of termination:

- The Customer is liable for payment for Services completed up to the termination date
- The Professional must deliver all work completed to date
- Marketplace cancellation and refund policies apply to any escrow funds held

---

## 9. Limitation of Liability

The Professional's total liability under this Contract shall not exceed the total fees paid under the Contract. Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.
