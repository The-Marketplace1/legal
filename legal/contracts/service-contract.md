## 3. Customer Responsibilities

The Customer agrees to:

3.1 Provide all information, access, and materials required for the Service Provider to perform the Services

3.2 Respond to requests for information within **2 business days**

3.3 Provide a safe and suitable environment for service delivery (where applicable)

3.4 Review and accept or dispute deliverables within the **48-hour acceptance window** as defined in the Marketplace Escrow Policy

3.5 Pay all agreed fees promptly through the Marketplace platform

---

## 4. Service Provider Responsibilities

The Service Provider agrees to:

4.1 Perform the Services with reasonable skill, care, and diligence

4.2 Meet the agreed timeline and milestones

4.3 Notify the Customer promptly of any issues that may affect delivery

4.4 Comply with all applicable laws and professional standards

4.5 Keep the Customer's confidential information strictly confidential

---

## 5. Changes to Scope

5.1 Any change to the agreed scope of Services must be agreed in writing by both parties through the Marketplace platform.

5.2 Scope changes may affect the agreed price and timeline. Any additional fees must be agreed before work commences.

5.3 Neither party may unilaterally expand or reduce the scope of the Services.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 **Customer-Specific Deliverables:** All deliverables created specifically for the Customer are assigned to the Customer upon full payment.

6.2 **Service Provider's Background IP:** The Service Provider retains ownership of pre-existing intellectual property. A non-exclusive licence to use such IP embedded in deliverables is granted to the Customer.

6.3 **Third-Party Content:** Where Services include third-party content or tools, the Service Provider must obtain and provide applicable licences for the Customer's use.

---

## 7. Warranties and Quality

7.1 The Service Provider warrants that the Services will be delivered in accordance with the agreed specification and industry standards.

7.2 If a deliverable does not meet the agreed acceptance criteria, the Customer may request revisions within the 48-hour acceptance window. The Service Provider will make reasonable corrections at no additional charge.

7.3 Revisions requested outside the acceptance window or beyond the agreed revision scope may be charged at the Service Provider's standard rate.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential information strictly confidential.

8.2 Confidential information may not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent.

8.3 This obligation survives termination of this Contract for **5 years**.

---

## 9. Liability

9.1 The Service Provider's total liability under this Contract shall not exceed the total fees paid.

9.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

9.3 Nothing in this Contract limits liability for fraud, personal injury, or death caused by negligence.

---

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate this Contract for material breach not remedied within **7 days** of written notice.

10.2 The Customer may terminate for convenience with **5 days' notice**, subject to payment for all Services completed and reasonable demobilisation costs.

10.3 Marketplace cancellation and refund policies apply to any escrowed funds on termination.

---

## 11. Dispute Resolution

Disputes are handled first through the Marketplace's dispute resolution process. If unresolved, escalation options are available as described in the Escalation Policy.
