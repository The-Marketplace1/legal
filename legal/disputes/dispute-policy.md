# Dispute Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Dispute Policy governs how the Marketplace handles disagreements between customers and professionals relating to services booked through the platform. It defines who can raise a dispute, on what grounds, and what process applies.

---

## 2. What Is a Dispute?

A dispute is a formal objection raised by a customer or professional through the Marketplace platform regarding a booked service. Disputes are distinct from general complaints and trigger the Marketplace's structured review process.

---

## 3. Grounds for Raising a Dispute

### 3.1 Customer Disputes

Customers may raise a dispute on the following grounds:

- **Non-delivery:** The service was not delivered at all
- **Partial delivery:** Only part of the agreed service was delivered
- **Material misrepresentation:** The service delivered was materially different from what was described or agreed
- **Unsafe conduct:** The professional engaged in conduct that created a safety risk
- **Unprofessional behaviour:** Conduct that violates the Marketplace's code of conduct
- **Payment discrepancy:** A charge differs from the agreed booking amount

### 3.2 Professional Disputes

Professionals may raise a dispute on the following grounds:

- **Non-acceptance:** The customer refuses to accept a fully completed service without valid reason
- **False or malicious complaint:** The customer raised a fraudulent or bad-faith dispute
- **Scope creep:** The customer demanded work beyond the agreed booking scope
- **Unsafe environment:** The service location presented unreasonable safety risks
- **Payment discrepancy:** Earnings do not reflect the agreed booking value

---

## 4. Eligibility and Time Limits

### 4.1 Customer Disputes

Disputes must be raised within **48 hours** of the service completion date or the agreed completion date, whichever is earlier.

Late disputes (after 48 hours) may be considered at the Marketplace's discretion in cases involving:

- Fraud or deception by the professional
- Safety-related incidents
- Evidence only discoverable after the acceptance window

### 4.2 Professional Disputes

Disputes must be raised within **48 hours** of the customer's acceptance (or deemed acceptance) of the service.

---

## 5. Evidence Requirements

Both parties are required to submit supporting evidence when raising or responding to a dispute. Relevant evidence may include:

| Evidence Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Visual documentation | Photos or videos of work completed or not completed |
| Written records | Booking details, chat messages, emails |
| Third-party confirmation | Witness statements, contractor sign-off |
| Receipts and invoices | Proof of materials purchased or costs incurred |
| Platform data | Booking records, timestamps, geolocation (where relevant) |

Failure to provide evidence does not automatically result in a decision against the non-submitting party, but it may affect the outcome.

---

## 6. Prohibited Dispute Conduct

The following are prohibited and may result in account suspension:

- Raising disputes in bad faith to avoid payment
- Submitting falsified or manipulated evidence
- Using the dispute process to threaten or coerce the other party
- Repeatedly raising disputes as a pattern of harassment
- Coordinating false disputes with third parties

---

## 7. Dispute Types

### 7.1 Service Disputes

Disagreements about whether the service was delivered as agreed.

### 7.2 Payment Disputes

Disagreements about the amount charged or earnings received.

### 7.3 Conduct Disputes

Disagreements arising from the behaviour of either party during the service.

### 7.4 Safety Disputes

Disputes involving allegations of unsafe conduct, harassment, or threats.

---

## 8. Dispute Resolution Process

The full dispute resolution procedure is described in [`resolution-process.md`](resolution-process.md). In summary:

1. **Initiation:** Dispute raised through the platform by either party
2. **Notification:** Both parties notified and invited to submit evidence
3. **Evidence Window:** 72-hour window for evidence submission
4. **Review:** Marketplace dispute team reviews all evidence
5. **Decision:** Resolution decision issued within 5 business days
6. **Action:** Funds released or refunded in accordance with decision
7. **Appeal:** Either party may appeal within 5 business days of the decision

---

## 9. Escrow During Disputes

All funds related to a disputed booking are held in escrow during the dispute process. No funds are released until:

- A resolution decision is issued, OR
- Both parties agree to a settlement

---

## 10. Dispute Outcomes

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Decided in customer's favour | Customer receives full or partial refund; professional bears the loss |
| Decided in professional's favour | Escrow released to professional; customer receives no refund |
| Mutually agreed settlement | Funds split as agreed; dispute closed |
| Insufficient evidence | Marketplace issues a balanced determination based on available information |

---

## 11. Repeat Disputes

Users with patterns of repeat disputes (as either claimant or respondent) may be subject to:

- Enhanced account monitoring
- Temporary suspension from the platform
- Permanent removal in cases of persistent abuse

---

## 12. Communication During Disputes

All dispute-related communication must occur through the Marketplace's official dispute interface. Direct contact between parties during an active dispute is discouraged and any such communication may be used as evidence.
