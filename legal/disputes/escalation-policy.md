# Escalation Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Escalation Policy defines the process for escalating unresolved disputes beyond the Marketplace's standard dispute resolution process. It applies when a dispute cannot be resolved through internal mechanisms and a party seeks further recourse.

---

## 2. When Escalation Applies

Escalation is available when:

- The internal dispute process has been completed and an appeal decision issued
- A party believes the final internal decision contains a material error of law or fact
- A dispute involves conduct that may constitute a criminal offence
- A regulatory complaint is warranted

Escalation is **not** a way to re-litigate disputes where the party simply disagrees with the outcome. The internal process must be fully exhausted first.

---

## 3. Internal Escalation Paths

Before external escalation, parties should use these internal escalation options:

### 3.1 Senior Review Request

After the standard appeal, either party may request a senior review if:

- New material evidence has emerged after the appeal decision
- There is a demonstrable procedural failure in the dispute process

**How to request:** Email escalations@marketplace.com with the booking reference and grounds for senior review.

**Timeline:** Response within 5 business days. Not all requests are accepted; acceptance criteria are applied at the Marketplace's discretion.

### 3.2 Executive Complaint

For systemic concerns about the dispute process itself (not the outcome of a specific dispute), complaints may be directed to:

**Email:** complaints@marketplace.com

This channel is for process feedback, not individual dispute outcomes.

---

## 4. External Escalation Options

### 4.1 Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

The Marketplace supports referral to an accredited Alternative Dispute Resolution provider where:

- The internal dispute process is exhausted
- Both parties consent to ADR, OR
- ADR is mandated under applicable consumer protection law

The Marketplace will cooperate fully with any ADR process and provide relevant booking records and evidence.

### 4.2 Regulatory Complaints

Users may raise complaints with relevant regulatory authorities, including:

| Region | Relevant Authority |
|---|---|
| European Union | National data protection authority (for data-related disputes); national consumer protection body |
| United Kingdom | Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) for data issues; Trading Standards for consumer disputes |
| Nigeria | Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC); Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) |
| Other regions | Applicable national consumer protection or financial services authority |

The Marketplace will cooperate with all regulatory investigations.

### 4.3 Legal Proceedings

Parties retain the right to pursue disputes through the courts of competent jurisdiction in accordance with applicable law. The Marketplace will comply with all lawful court orders.

**Note:** Initiating legal proceedings does not require the Marketplace to hold escrow funds indefinitely. Funds may be released in accordance with internal policy unless a court order directs otherwise.

---

## 5. Safety-Related Escalations

Disputes or incidents involving physical harm, sexual misconduct, or criminal conduct must be escalated immediately:

### 5.1 Emergency Situations

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (police, ambulance) first.

### 5.2 Reporting to the Marketplace

After immediate safety is secured, report to:

**Email:** safety@marketplace.com
**Urgent Safety Line:** Use the urgent safety flow in the Marketplace app where available; contact local emergency services first in an emergency

Safety incidents are handled by the Marketplace's dedicated Safety Team with priority response.

### 5.3 Law Enforcement Referral

The Marketplace will proactively refer incidents involving credible criminal conduct to law enforcement and will cooperate fully with investigations.

---

## 6. Payment-Related Escalations

For payment disputes not resolved through the standard process:

### 6.1 Chargebacks

Customers may initiate a chargeback with their bank or card issuer as a last resort after exhausting the internal process. The Marketplace will respond to chargeback requests with available evidence.

**Note:** Initiating a chargeback without first exhausting the internal process is a violation of the Payment Terms and may result in account suspension.

### 6.2 Financial Regulatory Complaints

Where a payment dispute involves suspected financial misconduct or regulatory violations, users may report to applicable financial services authorities.

---

## 7. Escalation Timelines

| Escalation Type | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Senior review request | Submitted within 5 business days of appeal decision |
| Regulatory complaint | Per the regulatory body's own procedures |
| ADR referral | As agreed between parties / per ADR provider rules |
| Legal proceedings | Per applicable limitation periods |

---

## 8. Marketplace Cooperation Commitment

Regardless of the escalation path chosen, the Marketplace commits to:

- Responding to all regulatory enquiries within required timeframes
- Providing relevant records and evidence when lawfully required
- Cooperating with ADR processes in good faith
- Complying with all valid court orders and regulatory decisions
