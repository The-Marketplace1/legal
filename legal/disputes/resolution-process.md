## 1. Introduction

This document defines the step-by-step process the Marketplace uses to resolve disputes between customers and professionals. All disputes are handled in accordance with the Dispute Policy.

## 3. Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Dispute Initiation

**Who:** Customer or professional
**How:** Through the platform (booking → raise dispute)
**When:** Within 48 hours of service completion or agreed completion date

When a dispute is initiated:

- The disputing party selects the grounds for the dispute
- They provide an initial statement and any available evidence
- Escrow funds are immediately frozen
- Both parties receive an automated notification

---

### Step 2: Notification and Evidence Window

**Duration:** 72 hours from dispute initiation

Both parties receive:

- A notification of the dispute
- A summary of the grounds raised
- A request to submit supporting evidence
- Guidance on what types of evidence are accepted

**Responding Party Actions:**

The non-disputing party must:

1. Acknowledge the dispute within 24 hours
2. Submit their evidence within 72 hours
3. Provide a written response to the claims raised

**Evidence Accepted:**

- Photos and videos (timestamped where possible)
- Platform messages and booking records
- Receipts, invoices, or materials lists
- Third-party statements
- Any other relevant documentation

**Evidence Not Accepted:**

- Hearsay or unverifiable claims
- Evidence submitted after the 72-hour window (except in exceptional circumstances)
- Altered or manipulated documents

---

### Step 3: Marketplace Review

**Duration:** Up to 5 business days from evidence window close

The Marketplace's Dispute Resolution Team:

1. Reviews all submitted evidence from both parties
2. Reviews booking records, messages, and platform data
3. May request additional information or clarification from either party
4. May consult with subject matter experts for specialised service categories
5. Applies the standards in the Dispute Policy to reach a determination

**Review Principles:**

- Both parties are treated impartially
- The burden of proof rests on the party making the claim
- Platform data (booking records, messages, timestamps) carries significant weight
- Where evidence is evenly balanced, the Marketplace may issue a balanced split determination

---

### Step 4: Decision

**Timeline:** Within 5 business days of the evidence window close

The Dispute Resolution Team issues a written decision that:

- States which party's position was upheld (or whether a compromise applies)
- Explains the key reasons for the decision
- States the financial outcome (refund amount, escrow release, or split)
- Notes any policy violations identified during review

**Possible Outcomes:**

| Outcome | Financial Effect |
|---|---|
| Fully in customer's favour | Full refund from escrow; no earnings to professional |
| Fully in professional's favour | Full escrow release to professional; no refund |
| Partial — customer | Partial refund; remainder released to professional |
| Partial — professional | Partial earnings released; partial refund to customer |
| Settlement | Per agreed terms between parties during mediation |

---

### Step 5: Action Implementation

Within 1 business day of the decision:

- Escrow funds are released in accordance with the decision
- Both parties are notified of the outcome and financial action
- Any policy violations are recorded and may trigger account review
- Refunds (where applicable) are processed per the Refund Policy timeline

---

### Step 6: Appeal

**Window:** 5 business days from decision notification

Either party may appeal the decision if they believe:

- New material evidence has come to light that was unavailable during the review
- A procedural error occurred during the dispute process
- The decision contains a factual error

**Appeals are not accepted for:**

- Disagreement with the weight assigned to evidence
- New arguments that could have been raised during the initial review
- Requests for a higher refund without supporting grounds

**How to Appeal:**

1. Submit an appeal through the platform dispute interface
2. State the specific grounds for appeal
3. Attach any new evidence (must be genuinely new, not previously withheld)
4. Appeals reviewed within 5 business days

---

### Step 7: Final Decision

**The appeal decision is final within the platform.**

If the appeal decision is unsatisfactory, the aggrieved party may escalate outside the platform as described in the Escalation Policy. The Marketplace will comply with valid orders from courts or recognised arbitration bodies.

---

## 4. Mediation Option

Before or during the formal dispute process, parties may opt for mediation:

- Both parties must consent to mediation
- A Marketplace mediator facilitates structured discussion
- Mediation may result in a mutually agreed settlement
- Agreed settlements are binding and not subject to further appeal

---

## 5. Safety Disputes

Disputes involving allegations of physical harm, harassment, or threatening behaviour are treated as priority disputes:

- Accounts involved may be suspended during investigation
- The safety team is involved alongside the dispute team
- Resolution may include account termination regardless of the financial outcome
- Relevant incidents may be referred to law enforcement
