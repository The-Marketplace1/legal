# Admin Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose and Confidentiality

This policy governs internal administrator access to Marketplace information and operational tools. It is an internal control document. Admin systems, permissions, investigation tools, and internal records must never be exposed to customers or professionals.

## 2. Admin Authority

Administrator authority is granted only for a documented business purpose and within the person's assigned role. No administrator may use access for personal benefit, curiosity, retaliation, or an unauthorised request from another person.

## 3. Access Principles

All admin access must follow:

- Least privilege
- Need to know
- Separation of duties
- Time-limited elevation for exceptional access
- Strong authentication and device controls required by Security
- Quarterly access review

## 4. Sensitive Actions

The following actions require elevated permission and an auditable reason:

- Viewing or exporting personal data
- Changing account status or professional verification
- Releasing, freezing, or adjusting funds
- Modifying dispute or safety outcomes
- Accessing communications or recordings
- Changing policies, compliance settings, or retention controls

Where feasible, sensitive actions require a second authorised reviewer.

## 5. Data Access

Administrators must view the minimum data necessary and must not download, copy, or disclose data outside approved systems. Bulk exports require Legal, Privacy, or Security approval depending on the data and purpose.

## 6. Audit Requirements

Admin activity must be logged with the administrator identity, action, timestamp, affected record category, and business reason where applicable. Audit records must be protected against alteration and retained according to the security and legal retention schedules.

## 7. Emergency Access

Emergency access may be granted to address an immediate safety, security, or service-integrity risk. It must be:

1. Approved by the on-call owner or Incident Commander
2. Limited to the smallest necessary scope
3. Logged at the time of access
4. Reviewed after the incident
5. Removed when the emergency ends

## 8. Violations

Unauthorised admin access may result in immediate access removal, investigation, disciplinary action, and regulatory or law-enforcement referral.
