# Company Policies

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the baseline internal policies that support lawful, ethical, and accountable operation of the Marketplace. It applies to directors, officers, employees, contractors, and authorised representatives.

## 2. Ethical Conduct

Everyone acting for the Marketplace must:

- Act honestly, fairly, and in the best interests of users and the organisation
- Comply with applicable law and approved internal policies
- Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them promptly
- Protect confidential information and personal data
- Keep accurate records and never falsify business information
- Raise concerns without fear of retaliation

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Personnel must disclose any personal, financial, family, or outside-business interest that could influence a Marketplace decision. The Legal or People Team determines mitigation, recusal, or management steps.

## 4. Anti-Bribery and Improper Influence

The Marketplace prohibits bribery, kickbacks, facilitation payments, and anything of value offered to improperly influence a decision. Gifts, hospitality, sponsorships, and charitable contributions must be reasonable, lawful, transparent, and recorded.

## 5. Records and Communications

Business records must be complete, accurate, and retained according to approved schedules. Personnel must use approved business channels for material decisions and must not delete or alter records subject to a legal hold.

## 6. Speak-Up and Non-Retaliation

Concerns about unlawful conduct, policy violations, safety risks, fraud, or retaliation may be raised to:

- manager or People Team
- legal@marketplace.com
- security@marketplace.com
- safety@marketplace.com

Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

## 7. Training and Accountability

Required compliance and data protection training is completed at onboarding and at the intervals set by the Compliance Team. Breaches of company policy may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to termination and referral to authorities.

## 8. Policy Ownership

The Legal and Compliance Team owns this document and reviews it at least annually or after a material legal or operational change.
