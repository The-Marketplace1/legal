# Internal Controls

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum internal controls used to protect Marketplace users, funds, information, legal obligations, and business records. Control owners must maintain evidence that controls operate as designed.

## 2. Control Domains

### 2.1 Access and Identity

- Access is approved by a role owner
- Privileged access uses multi-factor authentication
- Access is reviewed quarterly
- Departing personnel lose access promptly

### 2.2 Payments and Escrow

- Funds are reconciled against booking and payout records
- Refunds and manual adjustments require documented approval
- Disputed funds remain restricted until authorised resolution
- Segregation of duties is maintained between initiating and approving material adjustments

### 2.3 Professional Verification

- Verification criteria are documented
- Evidence is stored only in approved systems
- Expired licences are reviewed and restricted
- Adverse decisions have a review or appeal path where required

### 2.4 Data Protection

- Processing activities and processors are documented
- Privacy reviews occur before high-risk processing
- Data subject requests are tracked to completion
- Retention and deletion controls are tested

### 2.5 Security

- Vulnerabilities are triaged by severity
- Security events are logged and monitored
- Incident response exercises occur at least annually
- Backups and recovery controls are tested

### 2.6 Legal Change Management

- Policies have an owner, version, effective date, and review date
- Material changes receive legal approval before publication
- Superseded versions are archived and not silently overwritten
- Publication changes are communicated to affected teams and users

## 3. Control Evidence

Evidence may include approval records, review logs, training records, reconciliation reports, access reviews, processor assessments, incident reports, and policy sign-offs. Evidence must be complete, dated, attributable, and protected from unauthorised alteration.

## 4. Exceptions

An exception requires:

1. Written description of the control deviation
2. Risk assessment
3. Named owner and compensating control
4. Expiry or review date
5. Approval by the relevant control owner and Legal, Privacy, or Security where applicable

## 5. Testing and Remediation

Control owners perform periodic testing. Failed controls are recorded, assigned a severity, remediated, and retested. Material failures are reported to senior management and, where necessary, the Board or regulators.
