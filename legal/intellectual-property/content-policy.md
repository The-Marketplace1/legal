# Content Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Content Policy governs all content submitted to the Marketplace by users, including profile descriptions, service listings, reviews, messages, portfolio items, and any other user-generated content. It applies to all customers, professionals, and visitors.

---

## 2. Content Standards

All content on the Marketplace must be:

- **Accurate and truthful** — Content must not misrepresent facts, services, qualifications, or identity
- **Respectful** — Content must not demean, threaten, or abuse individuals or groups
- **Lawful** — Content must comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- **Relevant** — Content must relate to the platform's purpose of connecting service seekers and providers

---

## 3. Prohibited Content

The following categories of content are strictly prohibited on the Marketplace:

### 3.1 Illegal Content

- Content that facilitates, promotes, or involves illegal activities
- Offers or requests for unlicensed services in regulated sectors
- Content involving illegal weapons, drugs, or controlled substances
- Content that violates export control or sanctions laws

### 3.2 Harmful and Dangerous Content

- Content that poses a risk to user safety
- Instructions for self-harm or harm to others
- Content that glorifies or incites violence

### 3.3 Sexually Explicit Content

- Pornographic or sexually explicit material
- Services of a sexual nature (this platform does not support adult services)
- Sexual solicitation or harassment

### 3.4 Hateful and Discriminatory Content

- Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics
- Derogatory slurs or dehumanising language

### 3.5 Fraudulent and Deceptive Content

- False professional qualifications or credentials
- Fake reviews or ratings
- Impersonation of another person, business, or the Marketplace itself
- Misleading pricing or service descriptions
- Phishing attempts or social engineering content

### 3.6 Privacy Violations

- Sharing another person's personal information without their consent (doxxing)
- Images or recordings of individuals taken without their knowledge
- Content that violates data protection laws

### 3.7 Intellectual Property Violations

- Content that infringes third-party copyright, trademark, or other IP rights
- Plagiarised service descriptions or portfolio work

### 3.8 Spam and Unsolicited Communication

- Repeated, unsolicited promotional messages
- Bulk messaging or automated message campaigns
- Off-platform solicitation attempts via platform messaging

### 3.9 Child Safety

Any content that exploits, endangers, or sexualises minors is strictly prohibited and will be reported to law enforcement immediately.

---

## 4. Review and Rating Content

Reviews and ratings must:

- Relate to an actual, completed booking through the Marketplace
- Be based on the reviewer's genuine experience
- Not contain false statements of fact
- Not be submitted in exchange for payment, discounts, or other incentives
- Not be coordinated with others to manipulate platform ratings
- Not be used as a vehicle for threats or coercion

---

## 5. Content Moderation

### 5.1 Automated Moderation

The Marketplace uses automated tools to detect:

- Prohibited keywords and phrases
- Spam patterns
- Off-platform contact solicitation
- Suspected fraud signals

### 5.2 Human Review

Flagged content is reviewed by the Trust and Safety Team. Human review applies to:

- Reported content
- Appeals of automated moderation decisions
- Sensitive content categories

### 5.3 User Reporting

Any user may report content that appears to violate this Policy:

- Use the "Report" button on any profile, listing, review, or message
- Email: safety@marketplace.com

Reports are acknowledged within 24 hours and reviewed promptly.

---

## 6. Consequences of Policy Violations

Content violations may result in:

| Severity | Action |
|---|---|
| Minor / first offence | Content removed; warning issued |
| Moderate / repeat offence | Content removed; temporary suspension |
| Severe / illegal content | Content removed; permanent account termination; law enforcement referral |

The Marketplace reserves the right to determine the severity of any violation and the appropriate response.

---

## 7. Appeals

Users whose content has been removed may appeal the decision within 5 business days by contacting content-appeals@marketplace.com. Appeals are reviewed by a senior member of the Trust and Safety Team.

---

## 8. Platform Liability

The Marketplace is not the author of user-generated content and is not liable for content posted by users. However, the Marketplace will take action against content that violates this Policy upon becoming aware of it.
