## 5. Copyright Infringement Claims

If you believe that content on the Marketplace infringes your copyright, you may submit a takedown notice.

### 5.1 DMCA / Copyright Takedown Notice Requirements

Your notice must include:

1. A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
2. A description of the infringing material and its location on the Platform
3. Your contact information (name, address, email, phone number)
4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorised
5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorised to act on behalf of the copyright owner
6. Your physical or electronic signature

**Send takedown notices to:**
Email: copyright@marketplace.com

### 5.2 Our Response

Upon receipt of a valid takedown notice, the Marketplace will:

- Review the notice promptly
- Remove or restrict access to the allegedly infringing content if the claim appears valid
- Notify the user who posted the content
- Provide the user an opportunity to submit a counter-notice

### 5.3 Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice including:

1. Identification of the removed content and its location before removal
2. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the content was removed in error
3. Your contact information and consent to jurisdiction
4. Your signature

If a valid counter-notice is received, we may restore the content after 10 business days unless the original complainant initiates legal action.
