## 1. Introduction

This Trademark Policy explains how the Marketplace's trademarks, service marks, logos, and brand identifiers may and may not be used. It applies to all users, partners, professionals, and third parties.

---

## 2. Marketplace Trademarks

The Marketplace owns and maintains the following trademarks and brand assets ("Marketplace Marks"):

- The Marketplace name and wordmark
- The Marketplace logo and icon
- Associated taglines and product names
- Domain names and platform identifiers

These Marks are registered or pending registration in applicable jurisdictions.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses

The following uses of Marketplace Marks are permitted **without prior written consent**, subject to compliance with the usage guidelines below:

### 3.1 Factual Reference

You may use the Marketplace name to factually and accurately refer to the platform (e.g., "I offer services on the Marketplace" or "Book professionals through the Marketplace").

### 3.2 Press Coverage

Journalists and media may reference the Marketplace and its Marks in factual news coverage and editorial content, provided use is not misleading.

### 3.3 Platform Badges (Verified Partners)

Authorised partners provided with official "Partner" or "Verified" badges may display these on their materials in accordance with brand guidelines provided.

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are prohibited without prior written approval from the Marketplace:

4.1 Using Marketplace Marks as part of your own business name, product name, or logo

4.2 Modifying, distorting, recolouring, or creating derivative works of Marketplace logos or marks

4.3 Using Marketplace Marks in a way that implies partnership, endorsement, or affiliation not authorised in writing

4.4 Registering domain names, social media handles, or app names that incorporate Marketplace Marks

4.5 Using Marketplace Marks in advertising without authorisation

4.6 Using Marketplace Marks in a misleading or defamatory manner

4.7 Creating merchandise or goods that display Marketplace Marks without a written licence

---

## 5. Brand Usage Guidelines

Where use of Marketplace Marks is permitted or authorised:

- Always reproduce the Mark exactly as provided — do not alter fonts, colours, or proportions
- Maintain adequate clear space around the logo
- Do not place the logo on backgrounds that reduce legibility
- Always use the most current version of brand assets
- Do not combine Marketplace Marks with other logos in a way that implies co-ownership

Current brand guidelines and approved asset files are available by request from marketing@marketplace.com.

---

## 6. Trademark Infringement

Unauthorised use of Marketplace Marks constitutes trademark infringement and may result in:

- Takedown requests and cease-and-desist notices
- Legal action for infringement
- Claims for damages and legal costs

If you are unsure whether your intended use is permitted, contact legal@marketplace.com before proceeding.

---

## 7. Reporting Misuse

To report unauthorised use of Marketplace Marks:

**Email:** legal@marketplace.com

Include a description of the alleged misuse and, where possible, a link or image of the infringement.

---

## 8. User-Generated Content

Users who create content referencing the Marketplace (reviews, social posts, portfolio items) may use the Marketplace name in a purely descriptive and factual way. They may not use the Marketplace logo or other brand assets without written permission.
