# Escrow Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

The Marketplace operates an escrow system to protect both customers and professionals in all service transactions. This Escrow Policy explains how funds are held, when they are released, and how they are handled in the event of a dispute, cancellation, or refund.

---

## 2. Purpose of Escrow

The escrow system ensures:

- Customers are protected against non-delivery or unsatisfactory service
- Professionals are assured of payment upon legitimate service completion
- Disputes are handled fairly with funds held neutral during investigation
- The Marketplace acts as a neutral financial intermediary

---

## 3. How Escrow Works

### 3.1 Payment into Escrow

When a booking is confirmed:

1. The customer's payment is charged immediately
2. Funds are transferred into the Marketplace escrow account
3. The customer receives confirmation that funds are secured
4. The professional receives notification that a funded booking is confirmed

Funds held in escrow are not accessible to the professional until released per the conditions below.

### 3.2 Escrow Holding Period

Funds remain in escrow from the point of booking confirmation until one of the following events occurs:

- The customer accepts the completed service
- The 48-hour acceptance window expires without dispute
- A dispute is resolved and a release decision is made
- A cancellation and refund are processed

---

## 4. Escrow Release Conditions

### 4.1 Automatic Release

Funds are automatically released to the professional when:

- The customer marks the service as complete and accepted, OR
- 48 hours pass from the service completion date without a dispute being raised

### 4.2 Manual Release

The Marketplace may manually release funds to the professional when:

- A dispute is raised but resolved in the professional's favour
- The professional provides evidence of completed service delivery
- The Marketplace determines a customer dispute is unfounded

### 4.3 Partial Release

Partial release of escrow funds may occur when:

- A service is partially completed and the customer accepts the completed portion
- A dispute is settled with a partial payment agreed by both parties
- The Marketplace determines a fair split based on evidence reviewed

---

## 5. Refund from Escrow

Funds are returned to the customer from escrow when:

- The customer cancels the booking within the eligible refund window (see Refund Policy)
- The professional cancels the booking
- A dispute is resolved in the customer's favour
- The service is not delivered within the agreed timeframe
- The professional is suspended or removed before completing the service

Refunds from escrow are processed in accordance with the Refund Policy.

---

## 6. Dispute Handling and Escrow

### 6.1 Disputes During the Acceptance Window

If a customer raises a dispute within the 48-hour acceptance window:

1. Escrow funds are immediately frozen
2. Both parties are notified of the dispute
3. Evidence is collected from both sides
4. The Marketplace investigates and issues a resolution decision
5. Funds are released in accordance with the resolution (to the professional, to the customer, or split)

### 6.2 Late Disputes

Disputes raised after the 48-hour acceptance window may be considered at the Marketplace's discretion where there is evidence of:

- Fraudulent conduct by the professional
- Misrepresentation of services delivered
- Safety-related incidents

---

## 7. Professional Payout Rules

7.1 Released funds are credited to the professional's Marketplace earnings account.

7.2 Earnings can be withdrawn in accordance with the Withdrawal Policy, including minimum thresholds, processing times, and identity verification requirements.

7.3 The Marketplace deducts its service fee before crediting earnings to the professional's account.

7.4 Funds subject to open disputes or fraud investigations are withheld until resolution.

---

## 8. Escrow Account Management

8.1 Escrow funds are held in accounts segregated from the Marketplace's operating accounts.

8.2 The Marketplace does not earn interest on escrow funds. Where applicable law requires interest to be paid to the depositing party, the Marketplace will comply.

8.3 In the event of Marketplace insolvency, escrow funds held for users are not assets of the Marketplace and are protected from claims by Marketplace creditors, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

---

## 9. Multi-Milestone Services

For services structured with multiple milestones:

9.1 The full service amount may be held in escrow and released in tranches upon completion of each milestone, where both parties agree to this structure at booking.

9.2 Each milestone release follows the same acceptance window and dispute rules as a standard service.

9.3 Refunds for incomplete milestones are calculated on the proportionate value of the incomplete portion.

---

## 10. Escrow Fees

The Marketplace does not charge a separate escrow fee. Escrow management costs are included within the platform service fee.

---

## 11. Currency and Exchange Rate

Escrow funds are held in the currency collected at the time of booking. Any currency conversion for payout to a professional is subject to the exchange rate at the time of release, not at the time of collection.
