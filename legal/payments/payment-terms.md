## 1. Introduction

These Payment Terms govern all financial transactions conducted through the Marketplace platform. They apply to all customers, professionals, and any other parties transacting on the platform.

By making or receiving payment through the Marketplace, you agree to these Payment Terms.

---

## 2. Payment Responsibilities

### 2.1 Customer Payment Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Ensuring payment methods are valid, current, and have sufficient funds
- Authorising payment at the time of booking
- Paying for all confirmed bookings in full
- Resolving any payment failures promptly to avoid booking cancellation
- Paying any additional amounts agreed with the professional (through the platform only)

### 2.2 Professional Payment Responsibilities

Professionals are responsible for:

- Setting accurate and transparent prices in their service listings
- Not collecting payments from customers outside the Marketplace platform
- Providing banking or payout account details required for earnings disbursement
- Reporting any payment discrepancies promptly

## 3. Accepted Payment Methods

The Marketplace accepts the following payment methods (availability may vary by region):

- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Debit cards
- Bank transfers (where supported)
- Digital wallets (where integrated)
- Platform credits (where applicable)

The Marketplace reserves the right to add, remove, or modify accepted payment methods at any time.

---

## 4. Payment Processing

4.1 All payments are processed through the Marketplace's authorised payment processors. Full card details are not stored by the Marketplace; they are handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment providers.

4.2 Payments are charged in the currency displayed at the time of booking.

4.3 Exchange rate conversions (where applicable) are handled by the payment processor and may include a conversion fee.

4.4 The Marketplace may apply platform fees to transactions as disclosed at the time of booking.

---

## 5. Failed Payments

5.1 If a payment fails at the time of booking, the booking is not confirmed.

5.2 If a previously successful payment is reversed (chargeback), the Marketplace reserves the right to:

- Cancel the associated booking
- Suspend the customer account pending investigation
- Recover the reversed amount from any outstanding credits
- Pursue recovery through legal means if necessary

5.3 Customers are responsible for all chargeback fees incurred by the Marketplace as a result of fraudulent or unjustified chargebacks.

## 6. Platform Fees

6.1 The Marketplace charges the following fees (current rates are published on the platform):

| Fee Type | Who Pays | How Charged |
|---|---|---|
| Customer service fee | Customer | Added to booking total at checkout |
| Professional service fee | Professional | Deducted from earnings upon completion |
| Payment processing fee | Customer / Professional | Included in service fee where applicable |

6.2 Fee structures are subject to change. Updated fees apply to bookings made after the effective change date.

6.3 Promotional fee rates may apply from time to time and are governed by separate promotional terms.

## 7. Escrow

All customer payments are held in escrow by the Marketplace until the service is completed and accepted. See the Escrow Policy for full details including release conditions, dispute handling, and refund eligibility.

## 8. Payment Disputes

### 8.1 Platform Dispute Process

Payment disputes between customers and professionals must first be raised through the Marketplace's dispute resolution process. See the Dispute Policy for procedures.

### 8.2 Chargeback Restrictions

Customers agree to use the Marketplace's internal dispute resolution process before initiating a chargeback with their bank or card issuer. Initiating a chargeback without first attempting resolution through the platform is a violation of these Terms and may result in account suspension.

### 8.3 Fraudulent Transactions

Suspected fraudulent transactions must be reported immediately to fraud@marketplace.com. The Marketplace will investigate and take appropriate action, which may include account suspension and coordination with law enforcement.
