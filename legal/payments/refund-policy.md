# Refund Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Refund Policy defines the circumstances under which customers are entitled to refunds, the applicable refund amounts, and the timelines for processing refunds through the Marketplace.

---

## 2. Eligible Refunds

Customers may be eligible for a refund in the following circumstances:

| Scenario | Eligibility | Refund Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Booking cancelled by customer > 48 hours before service | Eligible | Full refund less processing fees |
| Booking cancelled by customer 24–48 hours before service | Eligible | 50% refund |
| Booking cancelled by customer < 24 hours before service | Not eligible (see exceptions) | None |
| Booking cancelled by professional at any time | Eligible | Full refund |
| Service not delivered | Eligible | Full refund |
| Service materially different from description | Eligible (dispute required) | Full or partial |
| Service partially completed | Eligible (dispute required) | Partial refund proportional to incomplete work |
| Dispute resolved in customer's favour | Eligible | Per resolution decision |
| Platform error causing duplicate charge | Eligible | Full amount of duplicate |

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

- Customer changes their mind after a service has been fully and satisfactorily delivered
- Customer fails to be present or available at the agreed time, resulting in non-delivery
- Customer provides inaccurate service requirements leading to scope mismatch, unless the professional failed to clarify ambiguities
- Customer dispute raised after the 48-hour acceptance window (unless exceptional circumstances apply)
- Services funded through promotional credits (unless the credit was applied in error)
- Payment processing fees (these are non-refundable in all cases)

---

## 4. Customer-Initiated Cancellations

### 4.1 Standard Cancellation Timeline

| Cancellation Timing | Refund |
|---|---|
| More than 48 hours before service | 100% (less non-refundable processing fee) |
| 24 to 48 hours before service | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours before service | 0% |

### 4.2 Emergency Exceptions

In cases of verified emergencies, the Marketplace may grant an exception to the standard timeline at its discretion. Qualifying emergencies include:

- Medical emergency (customer or immediate family member)
- Death of immediate family member
- Natural disaster or government-imposed restriction preventing service

Supporting documentation is required. Exceptions are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

---

## 5. Professional-Initiated Cancellations

If a professional cancels a confirmed booking for any reason, the customer receives a full refund within the standard processing timeframe. The customer will also receive priority rebooking assistance.

---

## 6. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:

- A service is partially delivered and partially accepted
- A dispute is resolved with a split determination
- A service deliverable is completed but ancillary elements are not

Partial refund amounts are determined by the Marketplace based on the proportion of the service completed, evidence provided, and the agreed scope.

---

## 7. Dispute-Based Refunds

Refunds arising from disputes are processed only after the dispute is resolved through the Marketplace's dispute resolution process. See the Dispute Policy for procedures.

- If resolved in the customer's favour: full or partial refund as determined
- If resolved in the professional's favour: no refund; escrow released to professional
- If settled: partial refund per settlement agreement

---

## 8. Refund Processing Timeline

| Refund Type | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Customer cancellation (within window) | 3–5 business days |
| Professional cancellation | 3–5 business days |
| Platform error refund | 1–3 business days |
| Dispute-resolved refund | 5–10 business days from resolution |
| Emergency exception refund | 5–10 business days from approval |

Processing times are from the date the refund is approved. Actual receipt of funds depends on your bank or payment provider.

---

## 9. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of booking. The Marketplace does not issue cash refunds or refunds to a different payment method unless:

- The original payment method is no longer valid
- The original payment was made with a card that has been cancelled

In such cases, the Marketplace will work with the customer to arrange an alternative refund method.

---

## 10. Platform Credits

In some circumstances, the Marketplace may offer platform credits instead of a cash refund. Credits:

- Are non-transferable and non-redeemable for cash
- Are valid for 12 months from date of issue
- Can be applied to future bookings on the Marketplace
- Are not subject to refund upon account closure

---

## 11. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. Log into your account
2. Navigate to the relevant booking
3. Select "Request Refund" or "Raise a Dispute"
4. Provide supporting information

Alternatively, contact: support@marketplace.com

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the booking completion date, except in dispute scenarios governed by the Dispute Policy timeline.
