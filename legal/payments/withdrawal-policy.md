# Withdrawal Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

This Withdrawal Policy governs how professionals may withdraw their earnings from the Marketplace. It defines eligibility requirements, processing timelines, supported payout methods, and applicable conditions.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Withdrawal

To withdraw earnings, professionals must:

2.1 Have completed identity verification as required by the Marketplace's verification programme

2.2 Have a valid, verified payout account (bank account or supported digital wallet)

2.3 Have available earnings that are not subject to an open dispute, fraud investigation, or compliance hold

2.4 Not have a suspended or restricted account

---

## 3. Available Balance

### 3.1 Pending vs. Available Earnings

Earnings become **available** for withdrawal after:

- The customer accepts the completed service, OR
- The 48-hour acceptance window expires without a dispute

Earnings remain **pending** while:

- The service is in progress or within the acceptance window
- A dispute is open and under review
- A fraud or compliance investigation is active

### 3.2 Minimum Withdrawal Threshold

The minimum withdrawal amount is **$20.00 USD** (or local currency equivalent). Earnings below this threshold accumulate until the minimum is reached.

The Marketplace reserves the right to adjust the minimum threshold with notice.

---

## 4. Supported Payout Methods

| Method | Availability | Typical Processing Time |
|---|---|---|
| Bank transfer (local) | All supported regions | 2–5 business days |
| Bank transfer (international) | Selected regions | 5–10 business days |
| Digital wallet | Selected regions and providers | 1–3 business days |

Supported payout methods vary by region. Your available options will be displayed in your account settings.

---

## 5. Processing Times

5.1 Withdrawal requests are processed on business days (Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays).

5.2 Requests submitted after 5:00 PM (platform time) are processed the following business day.

5.3 Processing times begin from the date the withdrawal request is approved, not the date it is submitted.

5.4 International bank transfers may take longer due to correspondent bank processing.

5.5 First-time withdrawals may require additional identity verification and may take longer to process.

---

## 6. Verification Requirements for Withdrawals

### 6.1 Standard Withdrawals

All withdrawals require:

- Verified identity (completed during account onboarding)
- Verified payout account (bank account number, sort code/routing number, or wallet ID)

### 6.2 Enhanced Verification for Large Withdrawals

Withdrawals exceeding the following thresholds may require enhanced verification:

| Threshold | Additional Requirement |
|---|---|
| Over $1,000 USD per withdrawal | Identity re-confirmation |
| Over $5,000 USD per calendar month | Enhanced due diligence documentation |
| First withdrawal to a new payout account | Account verification delay (1–3 business days) |

---

## 7. Banking Requirements

To receive bank transfers, professionals must provide:

7.1 **For local/domestic transfers:**
   - Account holder name (must match verified identity)
   - Bank account number
   - Sort code / routing number / SWIFT/BIC (as applicable)
   - Bank name and branch address

7.2 **For international transfers:**
   - All of the above
   - IBAN (where applicable)
   - Correspondent bank details (where required)

7.3 Account names must match the verified identity on file. Withdrawals to third-party accounts are not permitted.

---

## 8. Fees

8.1 The Marketplace does not charge a withdrawal fee for standard withdrawals.

8.2 Third-party banking or payment processing fees may apply and are deducted by the receiving bank or payment provider, not by the Marketplace.

8.3 Currency conversion fees (where applicable) are determined by the payment provider.

---

## 9. Currency

9.1 Earnings are denominated in the currency in which services were booked.

9.2 Withdrawals are processed in the currency of the professional's verified payout account, subject to currency conversion where applicable.

9.3 The Marketplace is not responsible for exchange rate fluctuations between the time of earning and the time of withdrawal.

---

## 10. Holds and Restrictions

The Marketplace may place a hold on withdrawal requests in the following circumstances:

- An open dispute or chargeback involving the professional's earnings
- Suspected fraud or irregular account activity
- Compliance review triggered by regulatory requirements
- Account suspension pending investigation

Professionals will be notified of any hold and the reason for it. Holds are lifted upon resolution of the underlying issue.

---

## 11. Failed Withdrawals

If a withdrawal fails due to incorrect banking details or a rejected transfer:

11.1 The funds are returned to the professional's Marketplace earnings account

11.2 The professional must update their payout account details and resubmit

11.3 The Marketplace is not liable for delays caused by incorrect banking information provided by the professional

---

## 12. Account Closure and Outstanding Earnings

Upon account closure:

12.1 Any available earnings will be paid out to the verified payout account on file within 14 business days

12.2 Earnings subject to open disputes will be held until resolved

12.3 Earnings cannot be withdrawn to a new payout account during account closure processing
