## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time so you don't have to keep re-entering them each time you visit or navigate between pages.

Similar technologies include:

- **Local storage:** Data stored in your browser that persists between sessions
- **Session storage:** Temporary data cleared when you close your browser
- **Pixel tags / web beacons:** Small images embedded in emails or pages used to track interactions
- **Device fingerprinting:** Technical attributes of your device used to identify it

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the platform to function. Without them, core services such as login, booking, and payment cannot operate.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `session_id` | Maintains your login session | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Prevents cross-site request forgery | Session |
| `user_prefs` | Stores essential UI preferences | 1 year |
| `geo_region` | Identifies your region for compliance | Session |

**Legal basis:** These cookies do not require consent as they are strictly necessary.

### 3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced features and personalisation, such as remembering your language preference or service area.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `language_pref` | Stores preferred language | 1 year |
| `currency_pref` | Stores preferred currency display | 1 year |
| `search_history` | Remembers recent searches | 30 days |
| `booking_draft` | Preserves in-progress booking details | 7 days |

**Legal basis:** Consent. You may disable these without losing core functionality.

### 3.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve our services.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_analytics_id` | Internal | Page visits and user journeys | 2 years |
| `_session_tracker` | Internal | Session behaviour analysis | Session |

**Legal basis:** Consent (or legitimate interests where permitted by applicable law).

### 3.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ad_id` | Third party | Ad targeting and frequency capping | 90 days |
| `_campaign_ref` | Third party | Campaign attribution | 30 days |

**Legal basis:** Consent. You may opt out of marketing cookies without affecting core services.

## 5. Cookie Consent

When you first visit the Marketplace, you will be shown a cookie consent banner. You may:

- **Accept all:** Enable all cookies including functional, analytics, and marketing
- **Accept necessary only:** Enable strictly necessary cookies only
- **Manage preferences:** Choose which categories to enable

Your preferences are saved for 12 months and can be updated at any time through the "Cookie Settings" link in the platform footer.

## 6. How to Manage Cookies

### 6.1 Platform Cookie Settings

Update your preferences at any time through: **Account Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences**

### 6.2 Browser Controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Options → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy, Search, and Services → Cookies

Note that blocking all cookies may prevent core platform features from working correctly.

### 6.3 Opt-Out Tools

For analytics and advertising cookies, you may also use:

- [Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/)
- [Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out](https://optout.aboutads.info/)
- [European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)
