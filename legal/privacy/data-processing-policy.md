# Data Processing Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This Data Processing Policy defines how the Marketplace collects, processes, stores, transfers, and deletes personal data on behalf of its users and in the course of its operations. It is an internal governance document that informs how all teams and systems must handle personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All Marketplace employees, contractors, and agents who process personal data
- All systems, applications, and services operated by the Marketplace that collect or handle personal data
- All third-party processors engaged by the Marketplace

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Personal Data** | Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person |
| **Data Subject** | The individual whose personal data is being processed |
| **Data Controller** | The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data |
| **Data Processor** | An entity that processes data on behalf of a controller |
| **Processing** | Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, transfer, or deletion |
| **Special Category Data** | Sensitive data such as health, biometric, racial, religious, or financial information |

---

## 4. Data Processing Principles

The Marketplace processes personal data in accordance with the following principles:

### 4.1 Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency

All processing must have a valid legal basis. Data subjects must be informed of how their data is processed through the Privacy Policy and relevant notices.

### 4.2 Purpose Limitation

Data collected for a specific purpose must not be used for incompatible purposes without additional consent or a new legal basis.

### 4.3 Data Minimisation

Only data that is adequate, relevant, and necessary for the specified purpose should be collected and processed.

### 4.4 Accuracy

Personal data must be kept accurate and up to date. Processes must be in place to correct inaccurate data promptly.

### 4.5 Storage Limitation

Personal data must be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the processing purpose, or as required by law. See Section 8 for retention schedules.

### 4.6 Integrity and Confidentiality

Personal data must be processed with appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction.

### 4.7 Accountability

The Marketplace is responsible for, and must be able to demonstrate, compliance with the above principles.

---

## 5. Legal Bases for Processing

All processing activities must be documented with one or more lawful bases:

| Legal Basis | When Used |
|---|---|
| **Consent** | Marketing emails, optional analytics, non-essential cookies |
| **Contract performance** | Account management, booking facilitation, payment processing |
| **Legal obligation** | Tax reporting, regulatory disclosures, fraud investigations |
| **Legitimate interests** | Fraud prevention, platform security, service analytics |
| **Vital interests** | Emergency safety situations |
| **Public task** | Where required by applicable law |

## 6. Data Categories and Processing Activities

### 6.1 Customer Data

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name, date of birth | Account registration | Contract |
| Contact | Email, phone | Communications, support | Contract |
| Location | Service address | Booking fulfilment | Contract |
| Financial | Payment method, transaction history | Payment processing | Contract |
| Behavioural | Platform activity, search history | Service improvement | Legitimate interests |

### 6.2 Professional Data

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name, government ID | Verification | Legal obligation; Contract |
| Financial | Bank account, earnings | Payouts | Contract |
| Qualifications | Licences, certifications | Verification | Legitimate interests |
| Performance | Ratings, reviews | Platform quality | Legitimate interests |
| Background check results | Where applicable | Platform safety | Legal obligation; Consent |

### 6.3 Special Category Data

The Marketplace processes special category data only where strictly necessary and with appropriate safeguards:

- Background check results (where required by law or service category)
- Health information disclosed in safety incidents

Processing of special category data requires explicit consent or an applicable exemption and must be documented in the processing register.

---

## 7. Third-Party Data Processors

The Marketplace engages third-party processors for specific activities. All processors must:

- Sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before receiving any personal data
- Process data only on documented instructions from the Marketplace
- Implement appropriate security measures
- Not sub-process data without prior written approval
- Notify the Marketplace of any data breach within 24 hours
- Delete or return data upon termination of the agreement

**Processor Register:** A current list of approved processors is maintained by the Legal and Engineering teams. Changes to the processor list require approval from the Data Protection Officer.

## 9. Data Subject Rights Management

Requests from data subjects must be:

1. Received through the designated privacy@marketplace.com channel or account settings
2. Logged in the Data Subject Request Register
3. Identity-verified before processing
4. Responded to within **30 days** (extendable to 90 days for complex requests with notice)
5. Documented with outcome and any decisions made

### Rights to be Honoured

- Access (copy of data held)
- Rectification (correction of inaccurate data)
- Erasure (deletion, subject to retention obligations)
- Restriction (pause processing pending review)
- Portability (machine-readable export)
- Objection (to legitimate interests processing or marketing)
- Withdraw consent (for consent-based processing)
- Rights related to automated decision-making

---

## 10. Data Breach Management

In the event of a personal data breach:

1. **Detection:** Breach is identified by any team member or automated system
2. **Notification:** Data Protection Officer and Security team notified within 1 hour
3. **Assessment:** Severity and scope assessed within 4 hours
4. **Regulatory Notification:** Reported to relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours if high risk
5. **User Notification:** Affected data subjects notified without undue delay if risk to rights
6. **Documentation:** Full breach record created regardless of severity

See `security/incident-response-policy.md` for detailed procedures.

---

## 11. International Data Transfers

When transferring personal data outside the originating jurisdiction:

- The transfer must be justified by an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs), or another recognised mechanism
- All transfer mechanisms must be documented in the processing register
- Recipients must implement equivalent data protection standards

---

## 12. Privacy by Design and Default

All new systems, features, or processing activities must undergo a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) if they:

- Involve new categories of personal data
- Introduce automated decision-making or profiling
- Involve sensitive or special category data
- Involve large-scale processing
- Create new data sharing arrangements

**Default settings** must be privacy-protective: only data strictly necessary for the specified purpose is processed by default.

---

## 13. Employee Obligations

All employees and contractors with access to personal data must:

- Complete data protection training at onboarding and annually thereafter
- Process data only for authorised purposes
- Report suspected breaches immediately to the Data Protection Officer
- Sign and comply with confidentiality agreements
- Not access personal data beyond what is required for their role

---

## 14. Policy Compliance and Review

This policy is reviewed annually and updated following:

- Changes in applicable law
- New processing activities
- Material changes to technology or operations
- Post-breach reviews

Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination and regulatory reporting.
