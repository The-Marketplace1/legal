## 1. Introduction

The Marketplace ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you use our platform, websites, and mobile applications (collectively, the "Services").

This policy applies to:

- Customers using the Marketplace to book services
- Professionals offering services on the Marketplace
- Visitors browsing our platform
- Business partners and vendors

Please read this policy carefully. By using the Marketplace, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Account Registration Data

When you create an account, we collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth (for age verification)
- Account security and authentication data handled by the authorised identity service
- Profile photograph (optional)
- Location / service area

### 3.2 Professional Verification Data

For professionals completing the verification programme, we additionally collect:

- Government-issued identity document
- Professional qualifications and certifications
- Business registration details (where applicable)
- Bank account details (for payment processing)
- Background check results (where required)

### 3.3 Booking and Transaction Data

When you book or provide services, we collect:

- Booking details and service descriptions
- Service location
- Payment information (processed via our payment provider; full card numbers are not stored by us)
- Transaction history
- Dispute and resolution records

### 3.4 Communication Data

We collect and store:

- Messages exchanged through the Marketplace platform
- Calls or audio communications made through our in-platform communication tools (subject to applicable law)
- Support tickets and correspondence with our team

### 3.5 Usage and Technical Data

We automatically collect:

- IP address
- Device type and operating system
- Browser type and version
- Pages visited, features used, and clickstream data
- Session duration and timestamp data
- Error logs and crash reports

### 3.6 Review and Rating Data

We collect:

- Reviews and ratings you submit about professionals or customers
- Responses to reviews
- Flagged content and moderation records

### 3.7 Cookies and Tracking

We collect data through cookies and similar tracking technologies as described in our Cookie Policy.

We use your personal data to:

## 6. Data Sharing

### 6.1 With Other Users

To facilitate services:

- Customer profile information (name, general location) is shared with the booked professional
- Professional profile information (name, qualifications, ratings) is displayed publicly on the platform

We do not share payment details, identity documents, or contact information beyond what is necessary.

### 6.2 With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the platform, including:

- Payment processors
- Cloud infrastructure providers
- Identity verification services
- Background check providers (where applicable)
- Communication and messaging providers
- Analytics providers
- Customer support platforms

All service providers are bound by data processing agreements and may only use your data for the specified purpose.

### 6.3 With Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose personal data to:

- Law enforcement agencies, courts, or regulators where required by law
- Regulatory bodies for compliance and reporting purposes
- Legal advisors in connection with legal proceedings

### 6.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same privacy protections described in this policy.

### 6.5 No Selling of Data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law:

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Account data | Duration of account + 7 years post-closure |
| Transaction records | 7 years from transaction date |
| Identity verification documents | Duration of account + 5 years |
| Communication records | 3 years from date of communication |
| Marketing preferences | Until consent withdrawn |
| Usage and analytics data | 2 years (aggregated indefinitely) |
| Dispute records | 7 years from resolution |

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

### 9.1 Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

### 9.2 Right to Rectification

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

### 9.3 Right to Erasure

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations and ongoing contract obligations.

### 9.4 Right to Restriction

You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.

### 9.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

### 9.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

### 9.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

### 9.8 How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit a data rights request to:

**Email:** privacy@marketplace.com

We will respond to all verified requests within 30 days (or as required by applicable law).

## 10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest
- Access controls and role-based permissions
- Regular security assessments and penetration testing
- Incident response procedures
- Employee data protection training

No system is completely secure. Please notify us immediately at security@marketplace.com if you believe your account has been compromised.
