## 1. Purpose

This register records approved changes to documents in the Marketplace legal repository. It supports accountability, auditability, regulatory review, and accurate identification of the version that applied at a particular time.

## 3. Record Requirements

Every new entry must include:

- Approval date and approver
- Exact document path and title
- Previous and new version
- Plain-language summary of the change
- Legal or regulatory reason
- Affected jurisdictions and user groups
- Effective date
- Publication and communication reference
- Link to the approved review record

## 4. Versioning Rules

- Increment the patch number for editorial corrections with no substantive meaning change
- Increment the minor number for substantive changes that do not replace the document's purpose
- Increment the major number for a new legal framework, materially different rights or obligations, or replacement document
- Never reuse a version number
- Keep superseded versions available in the approved archive

## 5. Review

The Legal Team reviews this register quarterly for completeness and reconciles it against publication records. Missing approval evidence is escalated to the Legal and Compliance lead.
