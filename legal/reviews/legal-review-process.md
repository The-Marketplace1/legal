# Legal Review Process

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

This process governs the drafting, review, approval, publication, and retirement of Marketplace legal documents. It ensures that policy changes are traceable, legally reviewed, versioned, and communicated to the teams and users they affect.

## 2. Change Categories

| Category | Examples | Minimum Review |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Typographical or formatting correction with no meaning change | Document owner |
| Minor policy | Clarification with no material user impact | Legal owner and affected team |
| Material policy | New obligation, fee, right, restriction, or data use | Legal, Compliance, affected owners, approval authority |
| Regulatory | Change required by law or regulator | Legal, DPO or specialist counsel, approval authority |
| Emergency | Immediate safety, security, or legal risk | On-call Legal approval, retrospective full review |

## 3. Submission Requirements

Every proposed change must identify:

- Document and section affected
- Reason for change
- Jurisdictions affected
- User groups and systems affected
- Legal or regulatory source
- Privacy, security, payment, safety, or operational impact
- Proposed effective date
- Required communication or consent action

## 4. Review Steps

1. **Intake:** Legal records the request and assigns an owner.
2. **Impact assessment:** The owner identifies affected policies, agreements, regions, teams, and users.
3. **Drafting:** A proposed revision is prepared using the approved template and preserves clear, plain language.
4. **Specialist review:** DPO, Security, Payments, Safety, Tax, or local counsel review where applicable.
5. **Stakeholder review:** Affected operational owners confirm the policy is implementable without changing its legal meaning.
6. **Approval:** The authorised approver signs off on the exact version to be published.
7. **Validation:** Structure, links, required files, cross-references, and version consistency are checked.
8. **Publication:** The approved document is published through the approved channel with its effective date.
9. **Communication:** Users and internal teams are notified where required.
10. **Archiving:** The superseded version and approval evidence are retained.

## 5. Approval Matrix

| Change | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Editorial | Document owner |
| Customer-facing terms or refund changes | Legal lead and business owner |
| Privacy or data protection change | Legal lead and DPO |
| Payment or escrow change | Legal lead and Finance/Payments owner |
| Safety or verification change | Legal lead and Trust & Safety owner |
| Security or incident change | Legal lead and Security owner |
| Governance or admin control change | Legal lead and executive owner |
| Material regulatory or risk change | Legal lead and designated executive/Board authority |

## 6. Publication Rules

- Only approved versions may be published
- The effective date must not precede final approval
- User-facing documents must be accessible in the languages and formats required by law
- Published copies must match the approved source exactly
- Material changes must not be silently substituted for an earlier version

## 7. Emergency Changes

An emergency change may be approved by the on-call Legal owner to address an immediate risk. The reason, scope, approver, and publication time must be recorded. A full retrospective review must occur within 10 business days.

## 8. Review Cadence

Core legal and privacy documents are reviewed at least annually. A review is also triggered by:

- New country or regulated service category
- Material product or business-model change
- New law, regulator guidance, or enforcement action
- Security incident, safety incident, or significant dispute
- Material change to a processor, payment provider, or communication provider
