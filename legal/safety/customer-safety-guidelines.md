**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

These Customer Safety Guidelines provide practical advice to help customers stay safe when booking and receiving services through the Marketplace. Following these guidelines helps protect you from fraud, ensures safe service interactions, and maximises your experience on the platform.

---

## 2. Before Booking

### 2.1 Use the Platform

Always book and pay for services through the Marketplace platform. Do not:

- Pay professionals directly in cash, bank transfer, or digital wallet before or instead of booking through the platform
- Arrange services with professionals you found through the platform via personal contact
- Respond to unsolicited offers from professionals to work outside the platform

Payments made outside the Marketplace are not protected by the escrow system and are not eligible for refunds or dispute resolution.

### 2.2 Review Profiles Carefully

Before booking a professional:

- Read their full profile, including qualifications, experience, and service description
- Review their ratings and customer feedback from previous bookings
- Check that the professional's listed services match your requirements
- Note any verification badges displayed on their profile

If a professional's profile seems incomplete, their ratings are unusually low, or their pricing seems too good to be true, consider an alternative.

### 2.3 Communicate Through the Platform

Before booking is confirmed, communicate with professionals through the Marketplace messaging system only. Be cautious about sharing your personal phone number, email address, or home address until the booking is confirmed.

---

## 3. During a Service

### 3.1 Service at Your Location

If a service is performed at your home or property:

- Confirm the professional's identity upon arrival (name, platform profile photo)
- You may ask to see professional identification or credentials for regulated services
- Ensure you or another responsible adult is present during the service
- Secure personal valuables, sensitive documents, and private areas not relevant to the service
- Keep the professional informed of any hazards or restrictions in your property

### 3.2 Verifying the Professional

If the person who arrives does not match the profile on the Marketplace, or if you have concerns about their identity:

- Do not allow them entry to your property
- Contact the professional through the Marketplace to clarify
- Report the situation to safety@marketplace.com immediately

### 3.3 Your Right to End a Service

You have the right to ask a professional to stop work and leave your property at any time if you feel unsafe, uncomfortable, or if the professional is behaving inappropriately. After the professional has left, report the situation through the platform.

## 4. Account Protection

### 4.1 Strong Passwords

Use a unique, strong password for your Marketplace account. Do not share your login credentials with anyone.

### 4.2 Phishing Awareness

Be alert to phishing attempts:

- The Marketplace will never ask for your password via email or message
- Always verify you are on the legitimate Marketplace website before logging in
- Be cautious of emails or messages that ask you to click links and enter your payment details
- Report suspicious communications to security@marketplace.com

### 4.3 Payment Security

- Only enter payment details on the official Marketplace checkout page
- The Marketplace uses encrypted payment processing — your full card number is never visible to professionals
- Do not share payment information in platform messages
- Review your payment receipts for accuracy after each booking

---

## 5. Reporting Unsafe Situations

### 5.1 During a Service

If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services (police, ambulance, fire) first.

After you are safe, report the incident to:
- **Email:** safety@marketplace.com
- **In-platform:** Report button on the booking

### 5.2 Reporting a Professional

Report a professional if they:

- Behave in a threatening, abusive, or inappropriate manner
- Fail to deliver the agreed service
- Attempt to collect payment outside the platform
- Ask for access beyond what the service requires
- Take, damage, or misuse your property

Use the "Report" button on the professional's profile or the relevant booking, or email safety@marketplace.com.

### 5.3 What Happens After You Report

The Trust and Safety Team reviews all reports:

- Urgent reports (safety risks) are escalated immediately
- Standard reports are reviewed within 24 hours
- You will receive a response within 3 business days in non-urgent cases
- Verified safety violations result in immediate account suspension or removal

## 6. Reviews and Feedback

Honest, fair reviews help other customers make informed decisions. After every booking, take a moment to leave an accurate review. Your feedback:

- Helps maintain quality standards on the platform
- Assists other customers in choosing the right professional
- Helps the Marketplace identify professionals who are not meeting standards

---

## 7. Scam Awareness

Common scams to watch out for:

| Scam Type | Warning Signs | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Off-platform payment | Professional asks for cash or direct transfer | Decline and report |
| Fake profile | Low reviews, vague profile, unusually cheap price | Book only verified professionals |
| Scope inflation | Professional demands extra payment mid-service | Do not pay; raise a dispute |
| Identity fraud | Person who arrives doesn't match the profile | Do not grant access; report |
| Phishing | Fake Marketplace email asking for login details | Do not click; report to security@ |

---

## 8. Children and Vulnerable Persons

If a service involves the home where children or vulnerable persons reside:

- Ensure an appropriate adult is always present during the service
- Brief the professional in advance about any special requirements
- Do not leave children or vulnerable adults alone with a professional
