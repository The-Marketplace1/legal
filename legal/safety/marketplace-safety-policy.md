# Marketplace Safety Policy

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

The safety of every person who uses the Marketplace — customers, professionals, and support staff — is a core responsibility of the Platform. This Marketplace Safety Policy defines how the Marketplace detects, prevents, and responds to safety risks across all transactions and interactions.

---

## 2. Safe Transactions

### 2.1 Payment Safety

All financial transactions must occur through the Marketplace's official payment system. The escrow model protects customers from fraud and professionals from non-payment.

The following are prohibited and flagged as potentially fraudulent:

- Requesting or accepting payment outside the Marketplace platform
- Requesting payment in cash, cryptocurrency, or gift cards
- Requesting advance payments directly to a personal account
- Sharing bank details in platform messages

### 2.2 Communication Safety

Users must communicate through the Marketplace's in-platform messaging system until a booking is confirmed. Sharing personal contact information (phone numbers, email addresses, social media) before booking confirmation is discouraged and may be restricted on the platform.

The platform monitors messages for safety risks including:

- Requests to circumvent platform payments
- Threatening or abusive language
- Solicitation of illegal services

---

## 3. Fraud Prevention

### 3.1 Account Verification

All professionals are subject to identity verification. Customers may be subject to payment verification. Fraudulent accounts are removed immediately upon detection.

### 3.2 Suspicious Activity Monitoring

The Marketplace uses automated and manual monitoring to detect:

- Unusual booking patterns
- Multiple accounts created by the same individual
- Repeated disputes or chargebacks associated with an account
- Off-platform solicitation attempts
- Misrepresentation in profiles or service descriptions

### 3.3 Reporting Suspected Fraud

Users who suspect fraud should report it immediately:

**Email:** fraud@marketplace.com
**In-platform:** Use the "Report" function on any profile, message, or listing

Reports are reviewed by the Trust and Safety Team within 24 hours.

---

## 4. Prohibited Behaviour

The following behaviours are prohibited on the Marketplace and will result in immediate suspension and possible permanent removal:

### 4.1 Fraudulent Conduct

- Creating fake profiles or impersonating another person or business
- Submitting fabricated qualifications or verification documents
- Soliciting advance payments outside the platform
- Using stolen payment credentials

### 4.2 Illegal Services

- Offering or requesting services that are illegal in the relevant jurisdiction
- Advertising services that facilitate illegal activity
- Using the platform to launder money or fund illegal activities

### 4.3 Harassment and Abuse

- Threatening, intimidating, or harassing other users
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate contact
- Discriminatory conduct based on race, gender, religion, disability, nationality, or other protected characteristics
- Stalking or unwanted contact outside the platform

### 4.4 Misrepresentation

- Misrepresenting professional qualifications, experience, or identity
- Providing false information during booking or verification
- Submitting manipulated or fabricated evidence in disputes

### 4.5 Platform Abuse

- Creating multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions
- Manipulating reviews or ratings
- Attempting to access another user's account without authorisation
- Introducing malware or harmful code into the platform

---

## 5. User Reporting

### 5.1 How to Report

Users can report safety concerns:

- **In-platform:** "Report" button on profiles, messages, and listings
- **Email:** safety@marketplace.com
- **Phone/chat:** Via the in-app support system for urgent matters

### 5.2 What Happens After You Report

1. Report is logged and reviewed within 24 hours
2. Urgent safety reports are escalated to the Safety Team immediately
3. The reported account may be suspended pending investigation
4. The reporting user is notified of the outcome where appropriate
5. Verified violations result in warnings, suspension, or permanent removal

### 5.3 False Reports

Submitting false safety reports is a violation of Platform policy and may result in the reporting user's account being sanctioned.

---

## 6. Platform Monitoring

The Marketplace conducts:

- Automated content moderation of platform messages
- Algorithmic review of booking patterns for fraud signals
- Manual review of flagged accounts and reports
- Periodic audits of professional profiles and verification records
- Post-booking safety checks following reported incidents

---

## 7. Incident Response

For safety incidents occurring during or in connection with a service:

1. Users should immediately contact local emergency services if in danger
2. Report the incident to safety@marketplace.com
3. The Safety Team will respond within 4 hours for urgent matters
4. Accounts involved may be suspended during investigation
5. Incidents involving criminal conduct may be referred to law enforcement

---

## 8. Children's Safety

The Marketplace does not permit:

- Account registration by persons under 18
- Services provided to or involving minors without explicit adult supervision arrangements

Any suspected exploitation or endangerment of minors is reported immediately to relevant law enforcement authorities.

---

## 9. Policy Review

This Safety Policy is reviewed and updated annually, and following any significant safety incident. The Marketplace may update this policy at any time to address emerging risks.
