# Professional Safety Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Introduction

These Professional Safety Guidelines provide practical guidance to help professionals stay safe when delivering services through the Marketplace. Safety is a shared responsibility — the Marketplace provides the framework, but professionals must take active steps to protect themselves.

---

## 2. Before a Service

### 2.1 Review the Booking Carefully

Before accepting a booking, review:

- The service description and scope — is it something you are qualified and equipped to deliver?
- The customer's profile, reviews, and any history on the platform
- The service location — is it a legitimate residential or commercial address?
- Any special requirements — are they reasonable and lawful?

Do not accept bookings that feel unclear, suspicious, or unsafe. You may decline any booking without penalty where there is a legitimate safety concern.

### 2.2 Communicate Through the Platform

Before a booking is confirmed, communicate through the Marketplace platform only. Avoid sharing your personal phone number, email, or home address until necessary for service delivery, and only do so after booking confirmation.

### 2.3 Verify the Location

For in-person services:

- Use mapping tools to verify the location is a legitimate address
- Note any access requirements or instructions in the booking
- If a location seems unusual or unsafe, contact the customer through the platform before attending

### 2.4 Share Your Plans

Before attending a service:

- Tell a trusted person where you are going, for how long, and the customer's name
- Share your estimated return time
- Leave emergency contact details accessible

---

## 3. During a Service

### 3.1 Arriving at the Location

- Arrive at the confirmed address only — do not follow instructions to an unannounced alternative location without confirming through the platform
- If the environment appears unsafe upon arrival, you are entitled to leave without completing the service and should report the situation to the Marketplace
- Do not enter a property if you feel unsafe or coerced

### 3.2 Customer Conduct

Professionals are entitled to a respectful working environment. If a customer:

- Acts in a threatening, aggressive, or abusive manner
- Makes inappropriate sexual remarks or advances
- Appears to be intoxicated or aggressive

You may leave the service immediately. Report the incident to safety@marketplace.com. You will not be penalised for leaving a genuinely unsafe situation.

### 3.3 Bringing Equipment and Tools

- Ensure all tools and equipment are in safe working order before attending
- Do not leave tools or equipment unattended in a customer's property
- Keep tools secure during transport

### 3.4 Unauthorised Individuals

- Do not bring unauthorised individuals (friends, family, subcontractors) to a service unless agreed with the customer in writing during the booking process
- If a customer has unauthorised individuals present in a way that makes you uncomfortable, you may raise a safety concern

---

## 4. Customer Interaction Rules

### 4.1 Professional Conduct

- Treat all customers with courtesy and respect regardless of the circumstances
- Maintain professional boundaries at all times
- Do not enter rooms or areas beyond what is necessary for the service
- Do not use, move, or take any customer property not related to the service

### 4.2 Privacy

- Do not photograph or record a customer's property or persons without explicit consent
- Do not share customer information, location, or details with third parties

### 4.3 Off-Platform Solicitation

Do not use personal contact details collected during a service to solicit off-platform business from customers. This violates the Professional Terms of Service and may result in account termination.

---

## 5. Reporting Unsafe Situations

### 5.1 During a Service

If you feel unsafe during a service:

1. Remove yourself from the situation if possible
2. Contact emergency services (police, ambulance) if there is immediate danger
3. Report the incident to safety@marketplace.com once you are safe

### 5.2 After a Service

Report any safety concerns after a service through:

- The platform's "Report" function on the booking
- Email: safety@marketplace.com

Include as much detail as possible: what occurred, the time, location, and any witnesses.

### 5.3 Reporting Abusive Reviews

If a customer leaves a review containing personal threats, false safety allegations, or attempts to coerce you through the review system, flag the review through the platform and contact disputes@marketplace.com.

---

## 6. Reporting Abuse

The Marketplace takes reports of professional abuse seriously. The following are reportable:

- Physical assault or threats of violence
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Racial, religious, or discriminatory abuse
- Theft of your property
- Deliberate damage to your equipment
- Defamation through false safety allegations

All reports are investigated by the Trust and Safety Team. Accounts found to have abused professionals are suspended or permanently removed.

---

## 7. Your Wellbeing

The Marketplace cares about professional wellbeing. If a service experience has affected your mental health or wellbeing:

- Take time before returning to the platform
- Contact our support team who can connect you with available resources
- Consider speaking with a mental health professional

The Marketplace does not penalise professionals for taking time away following a safety incident.
