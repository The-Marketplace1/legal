## Review Checklist

- [ ] Purpose is specific, explicit, and necessary
- [ ] Consent is separate from other terms
- [ ] No pre-ticked or implied consent
- [ ] Withdrawal is as easy as granting consent
- [ ] Notice contains required disclosures
- [ ] Consent evidence is retained and searchable
- [ ] Special-category processing received DPO approval, if applicable
