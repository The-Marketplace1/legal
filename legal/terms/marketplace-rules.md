# Marketplace Rules

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Reviewed:** 2024-01-01

---

## 1. Purpose

These Marketplace Rules set the minimum conduct standards for everyone using the Marketplace. They apply to customers, professionals, partners, vendors, and visitors, and supplement the Marketplace Terms of Service.

The Rules are designed to protect people, transactions, and the integrity of the platform. They do not replace applicable law or the more specific requirements in the Customer Terms, Professional Terms, Safety Policies, or Content Policy.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

No person may use the Marketplace to:

- Commit, facilitate, or solicit an illegal act or illegal service
- Defraud, deceive, impersonate, or misrepresent an identity, qualification, service, price, or result
- Harass, threaten, stalk, discriminate against, or abuse another person
- Exploit, endanger, or sexually target a child or vulnerable person
- Circumvent platform fees, escrow protections, verification, or account restrictions
- Manipulate ratings, reviews, search results, bookings, or referrals
- Launder money, finance terrorism, evade sanctions, or use stolen payment credentials
- Submit false evidence or make a bad-faith claim in a dispute
- Access, collect, disclose, or use another person's information without authorisation
- Upload malware, malicious code, or content intended to disrupt the platform
- Scrape, harvest, or commercially reuse Marketplace data without written permission
- Infringe copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other third-party rights

## 3. Off-Platform Transactions

Users must not move a transaction initiated through the Marketplace off-platform to avoid service fees or platform protections. The Marketplace may restrict messaging, cancel bookings, withhold funds subject to investigation, or suspend accounts where circumvention is suspected.

## 4. Regulated and Restricted Services

Professionals must not list a regulated service unless they hold every licence, registration, qualification, and insurance required in the service location. The Marketplace may restrict categories that require specialist legal, medical, financial, childcare, construction, or other regulatory controls.

## 5. Fair and Non-Discriminatory Access

Users must not refuse or condition service based on a protected characteristic, except where a lawful and documented exception applies. The Marketplace may investigate patterns of discriminatory conduct and take action under the Safety Policy.

## 6. Enforcement

Depending on severity and risk, enforcement may include:

1. Content removal or listing restriction
2. Warning and required corrective action
3. Reduced visibility or temporary feature restriction
4. Account suspension while an investigation is completed
5. Permanent account termination
6. Cancellation, refund, or withholding of funds under applicable policy
7. Referral to regulators or law enforcement

The Marketplace may act immediately where delay could create a safety, fraud, legal, or platform-integrity risk. Where appropriate, the affected person will receive notice and an opportunity to appeal.

## 7. Reporting

Report suspected violations through the platform or to:

- **Safety:** safety@marketplace.com
- **Fraud:** fraud@marketplace.com
- **Content:** safety@marketplace.com
- **Legal:** legal@marketplace.com

Reports are handled confidentially to the extent permitted by law. Knowingly false reports may themselves violate these Rules.
